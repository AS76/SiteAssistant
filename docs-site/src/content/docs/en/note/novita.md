This page collects the changes visible to those who use the application. It is not a technical changelog: for that, see the commit history in the repository.

## 21 November 2026

- **Separate permissions for individual hours and site attendance.** **Time bookings** shows source totals by company, day or period to members without **Worker names and individual hours** access. The separate **Site attendance** view shows names, shifts and scheduled times for the current operational day. Site owners can grant both permissions from **Module access**. See [Time bookings](../../moduli/prenotazioni-ore/) and [Users and roles](../../amministrazione/utenti-e-ruoli/#module-access).

- **New look.** The interface changes palette and type — paper, ink and brick, **Source Sans 3** for text and **Source Serif 4** for headings — and redesigns **Today** and **Diary**; **Tasks** and **Deviations** follow the same style. Navigation is a side rail above **1180 px**, a top bar between **640** and **1179 px**, and the six-item bottom bar below **640 px**. See [Mobile quick guide](../../guida/mobile/#the-bottom-bar) and [Diary](../../moduli/diario/).

- **Account and password in Settings.** The gear now opens a **Your account** section with name, email and user type for the selected site, plus the **Change password** and **Security** buttons. Anyone arriving from a temporary password finds the password change here too, without having to look for the banner at the top. See [Settings](../../guida/impostazioni/#your-account) and [Sign in and account](../../guida/accesso/#forgotten-or-temporary-password).

## 19 November 2026

- **Two pilot sites in the demo.** The demo now holds **Northline Piacenza** (`NL-IT-01`) and **Northline Midlands** (`NL-UK-01`): twin sites for an intralogistics plant, one with Italian data and one with English data. They are synthetic data built for the pilot, with tasks, diary, attendance, progress, deviations, HSE, fitness, tools and logistics. They contain no real data. See [Demo data](../../guida/dati-dimostrativi/).

## 18 November 2026

- **Quick status change from task cards.** On the board every card has a **⋯** button that opens the **Change status** menu: the dialog shows the current status and only the allowed moves, each with **Move to …**. When the move requires a note or a reason the usual dialog opens, otherwise the change is recorded straight away. Same permissions and same history as the detail. See [Tasks](../../moduli/attivita/#change-a-status-from-the-board).

- **Tasks link to the project structure.** The **New task** form has a **Project structure** selector, and in the detail the **Project structure** row shows the linked node with a **Link to WBS** command for owners and editors. Cards, filters and linked views show the node, or **Unlinked** when there is no link. See [Tasks](../../moduli/attivita/#link-a-task-to-the-wbs) and [Project structure](../../moduli/wbs/).

- **The project structure becomes operational.** The **Project structure** view is no longer read-only: nodes can be created and edited (**Create node**, **Edit**), local changes stay separate from imported values, and an import is reconciled without losing the work already done. See [Project structure](../../moduli/wbs/).

- **Today's quick actions speak differently.** The four commands are now **Verify tasks**, **Write diary**, **Take photo** and **Open progress**: the first opens the light task list already filtered to the ones awaiting verification, the last opens the Site progress view. The notice saying that Site progress and Deviations showed "only previously viewed data" is gone: resources load independently, with a per-resource error state and **Retry**. See [Mobile quick guide](../../guida/mobile/#the-today-screen).

- **Hours coverage on the Overview.** The **Hours booked in app** indicator shows the **h** suffix and, under the value, a coverage detail: displayed weeks, full-period hours, hours outside the six-week window and source coverage over days and hours. When the source does not record worked hours, the indicator says coverage cannot be verified. See [Common functions](../../guida/funzioni-comuni/#reading-the-overview).

## 17 November 2026

- **Sign-in with a passkey.** The **Sign in** form offers two passkey commands: **Sign in with a passkey**, next to **Sign in**, which uses the email written in the field, and **Use a passkey without email**, below the form, which lets the browser choose among the passkeys saved for the site. The confirmation happens in the browser — fingerprint, face, PIN or security key — and the session lasts like the others: **7 days**. If the browser does not support passkeys, or the page is not open over a secure connection, the two buttons stay disabled with a note asking you to use your password; if passkeys are not configured on the server the application says so. The password stays: whoever does not register a passkey keeps signing in as before. See [Sign in and account](../../guida/accesso/#signing-in-with-a-passkey).

- **Passkeys are managed from the Security window.** In the **Your account** section of **Settings** the **Security** button opens the account's passkeys, with name, creation date and last use, or **Never used**. From there you add a passkey, rename it and delete the one you no longer need, with a confirmation: deleting it revokes the sessions opened with that passkey. The last remaining passkey cannot be deleted, because without it you could not sign back in without a password. See [Sign in and account](../../guida/accesso/#your-passkeys) and [Settings](../../guida/impostazioni/#security-your-passkeys).

- **Registering a passkey rechecks the session.** Registering a passkey requires the session that opened the **Security** window to still be valid: if it has been revoked or has expired in the meantime, the passkey is not registered and the application shows the message, without taking you back to the sign-in screen. The same check applies to renaming and deleting a passkey. See [Sign in and account](../../guida/accesso/#your-passkeys).

## 15 November 2026

- **The assistant has a button in the header too.** After signing in the **AI** button now appears in two places: in the top bar, just before the gear, and at the bottom right as before. Both open the same **Data assistant** panel, with the same conversation and suggestions; the **×** closes it and returns focus to the AI button for the current width. They do not appear on the sign-in screen. See [Data assistant](../../guida/assistente-ai/).

- **Number separators can follow the language.** **Settings** now offers three **Separators** choices: **Automatic** — the new default, following the interface language —, IT and EN. With **Automatic** numbers switch from `1.234,56` to `1,234.56` together with the IT/EN selector; choosing IT or EN keeps the format on that choice. Preferences saved by a previous version restart from **Automatic**: whoever had chosen IT or EN sets it again. See [Settings](../../guida/impostazioni/).

- **Receipts only on shipped lines.** The **Record receipt** window now lists only the ordered lines actually contained in the shipment's packages, not every line of the orders involved: an ordered line that is not packed no longer appears. A shipment already **Received** no longer shows **Advance status** or **Record receipt**, and whoever opens the window from a detail that is not up to date reads “The status transition is not allowed or requires a receipt. Refresh the detail.”. See [Procurement](../../moduli/logistica/#recording-a-receipt).

- **Dates are read as days.** The dates of orders, packages, shipments and receipts, and the **Diary**'s operational day, appear as a day in the interface language — `15 Nov 2026` in English, `15 nov 2026` in Italian — instead of the `2026-11-15` form. Next to the Diary's date field the date is repeated in full. The saved value does not change: it stays in ISO format. See [Procurement](../../moduli/logistica/#reading-the-orders) and [Site diary](../../moduli/diario/#recording-a-contribution).

- **Phone zoom stays put.** The application blocks two-finger zoom and the browser's multi-touch gestures: the page stays at the scale it is drawn at, while scrolling, dragging and tapping stay native. This is deliberate, so cards, date fields and full-width commands fill the available width. See [Mobile quick guide](../../guida/mobile/#zoom-stays-put).

- **Esc does not throw away a filled-in window.** In the input and edit windows the **Esc** key closes only when you have not touched anything: with filled-in fields the window stays open and leaving takes the **×** or **Cancel**. On opening, focus goes to the first usable field and, when the window holds a destructive command such as **Delete**, to the **Cancel** button, so **Enter** does not confirm the deletion. See [Common features](../../guida/funzioni-comuni/#accessibility-and-devices).

- **The navigation groups open with more room.** On the desktop, on the first visit with no saved choice, every group is open only when the window is at least **1000 px** tall, no longer 800: below that only the group of the current view stays open. See [Common features](../../guida/funzioni-comuni/#the-navigation-groups).
- **Reporting for imported bundles.** The **Management** group has the **Reporting** entry: a read-only summary of the latest complete bundle for each project scope, with project reference, declared export date, load date and time, counts by family — employees, work days, work schedule, progress items, daily logs and deviations — total rows and quality issues. The view exposes counts only, with no detail rows and no personal data, and does not depend on the selected site. It refreshes with **Refresh**; when the reporting database connection is not configured on the server it says so, and without a complete bundle it stays empty. See [Reporting](../../moduli/reporting/).

- **Shipments in Procurement.** The **Procurement** view now has three tabs, **Orders**, **Packages** and **Shipments**, and the choice lasts for the browser session. The **Shipments** tab lists the site's shipments with number, carrier, departure, expected arrival, actual arrival, status and package count, with the **Status** filter; the number opens the detail with its packages and receipts. Owners and editors create a shipment with carrier, dates and at least one package among those not yet assigned, advance the status from **Planned** to **In transit**, **Arrived** and **Received** one step at a time, and record receipts of an ordered line with the quantity that arrived and a note. Moving to **Received** needs at least one receipt, and a package can be in one shipment only. The creation date of packages and receipts is read as a day, without a time. See [Procurement](../../moduli/logistica/#shipments).

- **Packages in Procurement.** The **Procurement** view now has two tabs, **Orders** and **Packages**, and the choice lasts for the browser session. The **Packages** tab lists the site's packages with number, order, status, line count, packed quantity and creation date, with the **Status** and **Order** filters; the number opens the detail with the packed lines. Owners and editors create a package by choosing the order — also from the order detail, with **Create package** — and entering, line by line, the quantity to pack next to the ordered and already packed quantities; the number is optional, because when it is missing the service assigns the first free one as `PKG-001`. The sum with what is already packed cannot exceed the ordered quantity, the first package that packs a still open line moves it into execution, and a package is closed with a confirmation, after which it no longer changes. See [Procurement](../../moduli/logistica/#packages).

- **Procurement is on screen.** The **Management** group has the **Procurement** entry: the site's purchase orders with number, supplier, date, status, line count and total quantity, the **Status** and **Supplier** filters and, opening the number, the detail with its lines — material number, description, unit, quantity, WBS item, status, execution start and completion date. Owners and editors create orders and lines and change status: lines move from **Open** to **In execution** and then to **Completed**, or to **Not relevant**, and the order follows its lines, moving into execution as soon as one line starts. The total quantity **sums quantities without converting units**. The `logistics` module stays off by default and is granted in the **Procurement** column of **Module access**; without the module the entry does not appear in the navigation. The demo data has two orders per site. See [Procurement](../../moduli/logistica/).

- **Packages and shipments, API first.** Ordered lines can be grouped into **packages**, with the packed quantity of each line: the sum across packages cannot exceed the ordered quantity, the first package that packs a still open line moves it into execution with the day's date, and a package is closed with a command. Packages are grouped into **shipments** with carrier, expected dates and four statuses — planned, in transit, arrived, received — that advance one step at a time; a shipment is received only after at least one receipt, which records how much of a line arrived. Packages are now seen and created from the **Packages** tab of the **Procurement** view (see **Packages in Procurement** above); shipments have the **Shipments** tab of the same view (see **Shipments in Procurement** above), with the `logistics` module. See [REST API](../../riferimento/api/#packages) and [REST API](../../riferimento/api/#shipments).

- **The demo is bilingual, and it says so.** Each of the four demo sites now has a coherent story and eight weeks of data: **Borgo Nuvolario** (FITT-01) and **Ponte dei Sogni Blu** (FITT-02) in Italian, **Mille Avenue** (FITT-03) and **Levante Warehouse** (FITT-04) in English, with forty diary days per site. At the top of the **Overview**, and under the **DEMO** bar on the sign-in screen, a note says which sites are in Italian and which in English; the **IT/EN** selector still translates the interface only. For those who administer the environment, resetting the demo data no longer deletes the sites or the memberships. These are test data: do not enter confidential information. See [Common features](../../guida/funzioni-comuni/#language) and [Sign in and account](../../guida/accesso/).

- **Purchase orders, API first.** A site can record purchase orders — number, supplier, date and notes — and their lines: description, material code, unit, quantity and a link to a project structure item. Order number and line number are unique; the order status follows its lines — in execution as soon as one line starts, completed when all are closed or not relevant — and lines move from open to in execution to completed, or to not relevant, with the date of the step. Every write leaves a row in the audit register, with the optional note of transitions. The view arrives the same day: see **Procurement is on screen** above. The endpoints require the `logistics` module, off by default and grantable from the **Module access** table or through the API. See [REST API](../../riferimento/api/#purchase-orders) and [Users and roles](../../amministrazione/utenti-e-ruoli/#module-access).

- **The HSE demo has subjects and inspections, and the views that load two lists show their data again.** The demo environment now creates, for the four sites, the subjects to inspect with their inspections, different outcomes and due dates: the **Inspections** view opens with real data and shows all five states — **Compliant**, **Due soon**, **Overdue**, **Not compliant** and **Never inspected**. The **Deviations**, **Site progress**, **Time bookings** and **Fitness** views, which load two lists, show their data again instead of staying on “Loading…”. These are test data: do not enter confidential information. See [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#the-inspections-view).

- **From the register, one command reaches the inspection.** Rows of the **Register** born from an inspection — type **Inspections** and **External visits** — carry **Open the inspection** in the **Type** cell: it opens the subject page in the **Inspections** view with that inspection's checks already open, and the **Register** / **Inspections** choice stays on **Inspections** for the session. Rows entered by hand have no command, because they have no inspection behind them. See [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#reading-the-register).

- **A photo on the inspection, under the same rules as events.** Opening an inspection on the subject page shows **Attachments** below the checks: images appear as thumbnails with the file name and open full size in a window, while other files stay a row with **Open attachment**. Owners and editors upload with **Add photo** — images or PDFs up to 15 MiB — and the usual rule applies: photograph the condition, not the people. Attachments are seen only from the inspection of the same site and, as for events, every opening writes a **Read** row in the audit register, readable through the API by owners and verifiers. See [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#attachments-on-an-inspection).

- **HSE inspections are recorded from the subject page.** On the page of a subject, owners and editors find **New inspection**: the window loads the family checklist and asks for date, performer, role, mode — internal or external visit — and outcome, with one button per check among **Compliant**, **Not compliant** and **Not applicable**. The proposed outcome is **Compliant** and switches by itself to **Compliant with notes** when a check fails, until you choose it; on failed checks the note for that check opens. A line tells how many checks are filled in and saving stays off while one is missing. Once saved, the page refreshes and the message gives the next due date, computed from the template interval; as before the inspection is neither edited nor deleted, and recording it needs a connection. See [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#recording-an-inspection).

- **The subject page for HSE: state, history and every check.** In the **Inspections** view the name of a subject opens its page: name, code, template family with its reference and the number of checks, the state with the same icon as the list, the last inspection and the next due date, with a notice when the template checklist is still a draft. Below, **Inspection history** lists the inspections from the most recent with date, performer, role, mode — internal or external visit —, outcome and the tally of compliant, not compliant and not applicable checks; opening the date shows the stored checks, in template order, each with the result written in words and its own note. The **Back to inspections** button returns to the list with the filters and order as you left them. See [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#opening-a-subject-state-history-and-checks).

## 14 November 2026

- **HSE subjects are created and edited from the screen.** In the **Inspections** view owners and editors find **New subject** — also when the list is empty — and the **Edit** command on every row: name, code, template family and notes are entered there, and when editing also the **Active** state, which when switched off keeps the subject in the list with the **Inactive** badge. A name already used in the site is rejected under the **Name** field, and the family is chosen among the templates of the site's jurisdiction. In the same release, setting up HSE assets and recording inspections move from owners and verifiers to **owners and editors**, the same rule as the rest of HSE; deleting a subject stays API only. See [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#adding-or-editing-a-subject).

- **The HSE Inspections view: subjects, deadlines and status at a glance.** The HSE section now has two screens: next to the **Register** sits **Inspections**, listing the subjects to inspect with name and code, template family, last inspection, next due date and status, with **Category** and **Status** filters and three orders — next due date ascending (the default, rows without a due date at the bottom), name ascending, last inspection descending. The status is **In order**, **Due soon** within 30 days, **Overdue**, **Failed** or **Never inspected**, with an icon next to the text; the count line gives how many subjects there are and only the states that have a number. The screen is read only and the Register/Inspections choice lasts for the browser session. In the same release the register's **Type** filter also offers **Inspections** and **External visits**, while the **New HSE event** form stays on the three manual types: those two types come from inspections only, and typing them by hand would create a row with no checks behind it. See [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#the-inspections-view).

- **HSE inspections reach the register, and the asset list says what needs attention, API only for now.** Recording an asset inspection now also writes a row in the HSE register: type `inspection` for an internal check, `external_visit` for an external one, with severity High when the outcome is failed, Medium for passed with notes and Low for passed; the location is the asset code, or its name when the code is missing, and the notes carry the asset and the template. Register row and inspection are created in the same transaction: if either write fails nothing is left behind, and the inspection keeps the row identifier as `hse_event_id`. The register summary counts the two new types, always present in the counts even at zero; the asset list returns for every row the date of the latest inspection, its outcome, the next due date and a status — never inspected, failed, overdue, due within 30 days or in order. The manual event form stays on the three manual types; the **Type** filter now also offers inspections and external visits (see **The HSE Inspections view** under the same date). See [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#assets-and-inspection-templates) and [REST API](../../riferimento/api/#hse-inspections).

- **HSE assets and inspection templates, API only for now.** A site can now list the **HSE assets** to inspect — tools, site works and personal protective equipment — with a name unique within the site, a code, notes, a link to a tool in the register and an active flag; the list is ordered by category and name. Every asset starts from an **inspection template** for the site's jurisdiction: the catalogue holds seven families, three Italian (lifting, PPE, scaffolding) and four British (lifting, lifting accessories, PPE, scaffolding), with the expected visual checks and interval. The templates are still drafts and there is no dedicated screen: assets and catalogue are read and written through the API only, where every site role reads and owners and verifiers write. See [REST API](../../riferimento/api/#hse-assets-and-the-inspection-catalogue) and [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#assets-and-inspection-templates).

- **HSE inspections, API only for now.** An inspection records the date, the person who performed it, whether the check was internal or external, the outcome — passed, passed with notes, failed — and the answer to every check in the asset's template, with the next due date computed from the template interval and the day clamped to the last of the month when it does not exist. Every template item is mandatory and must be answered once; the inspection keeps the checks as they were that day, so changing the template does not rewrite past inspections. It is neither edited nor deleted: a correction is a new inspection. Like the asset inventory, it is recorded and read through the API only, where every site role reads and owners and verifiers write. See [REST API](../../riferimento/api/#hse-inspections) and [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#assets-and-inspection-templates).

- **Every site declares its jurisdiction.** A site now carries a `jurisdiction` attribute, `IT` or `UK`: it is the law that applies to the site and does not depend on the interface language. Existing sites and sites created without it stay `IT`. The value is read through the site APIs and changed only through the API, by an owner, with `PATCH /api/sites/{id}`, which leaves a row in the audit register; the interface does not offer the choice yet. See [REST API](../../riferimento/api/#sites) and [Sites](../../moduli/cantieri/#site-jurisdiction).
- **Every dropdown is easy to tap.** On a touch device all dropdown menus are now at least **44 px** tall, not only the site selector: filters, sorting, forms and settings. Nothing changes on the desktop. See [Mobile quick guide](../../guida/mobile/#touch-targets).
- **Visual audit clean-up.** Three behaviour changes and a tidy-up. **Settings** no longer offer a **Decimals** choice: amounts always have two decimals, hours one decimal, counts none, and the chosen **Separators** apply to every number, not only to amounts. In **Deviations** the **To reconcile** panel on a phone moves **below** the board or the list, so filters and deviations stay at the top. In **HSE** the **Edit** and **Delete** commands of each row stay on one line, with a tap area of at least 44 px. The “Date: day/month/year” hint under date fields is gone: the field shows the date in the browser language while the saved value stays in ISO format. Small text — badges, notes, table headings — is a little larger. See [Settings](../../guida/impostazioni/), [Deviations](../../moduli/deviazioni/#to-reconcile) and [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#reading-the-register).
- **The deviations board in four groups.** The columns per single status become four groups: **Open** (`open` and `approved`), **In progress**, **To close** (work completed) and **Closed** (`closed` and `rejected`). Every card still shows its exact official status, and groups with an unrecognised status stay apart under **Unmapped status**. When the board is wider than the window the hint **scroll ↔** appears, with the edge faded on the side where more content is available. See [Deviations](../../moduli/deviazioni/#consulting-deviations).
- **Priorities in the interface language.** The priorities imported with deviations — `low`, `medium`, `normal`, `high`, `urgent` — now appear as **Low**, **Medium**, **Normal**, **High**, **Urgent** on cards, in the list, in the detail and in the **Priority** filter, and switch to Bassa, Media, Normale, Alta, Urgente with the **IT** selector. A value not in the list stays as it is in the imported data. See [Deviations](../../moduli/deviazioni/#consulting-deviations).
- **Light tasks as cards on a phone.** Below 800 px wide the **Light tasks** table becomes one card per task, with the label beside every field and the **Close** or **Reopen** command at full width, at least 44 px tall. Data, order and review rules do not change. See [Tasks and reviews](../../moduli/attivita/#light-tasks) and [Mobile quick guide](../../guida/mobile/#cards-instead-of-tables).

## 13 November 2026

- **Attachments on HSE events, with a read trail.** A saved event accepts photos or PDFs up to 15 MiB from the **Attachments** section of the edit window. The rule is “photograph the condition, not the people”: no identifiable faces and no health data, with a diagram or pictogram as an alternative to a photo; the application warns when the file name or description suggests a person or an injury, but it does not analyse the image and does not block the upload. Every opening of an HSE attachment writes a **Read** row in **Latest changes**, with the file name; deleting the event stops serving its attachments. See [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#attachments-on-an-event) and [REST API](../../riferimento/api/#attachments).

- **Four demo sites with different stories.** The demo environment now holds four sites — FITT-01, FITT-02, FITT-03 and FITT-04 — instead of two: a large one well advanced, a bridge just started, a small site only recently opened and one already finished. Each has its own **HSE** register and its own **Diary** entries, and whoever sees FITT-01 sees the others with the same role. These are test data: do not enter confidential information.
- **Collapsible side navigation and Today on the desktop.** On the desktop the side bar opens with the **Today** entry above the groups; each group — **Operations**, **Site**, **Management** — opens and closes when you click its heading, and the **▸** / **▾** arrow shows the state. The application remembers in that browser which groups you left open, opens by itself the group of the view you are opening and, on the first visit, keeps every group open only when the window is at least 800 px tall. See [Common features](../../guida/funzioni-comuni/#the-navigation-groups).
- **More readable secondary text, green focus ring.** Secondary text — subtitles, notes, table headings — has a higher contrast on the light background; the focus ring on buttons, links, fields and collapsible headings is now green with a white halo, instead of orange. See [Common features](../../guida/funzioni-comuni/).
- **Date fields with a hint, easier to tap.** Every date field — the diary's operational day, the HSE **From** / **To** filters and the dates in the input forms — shows the hint “Date: day/month/year” under the field, tied to the field for screen readers; the saved value stays in ISO format. On a touch device date fields are at least **44 px** tall and below **520 px** wide the field text is **16 px**, so the phone browser no longer zooms the page when you focus it. In the **HSE** view the **From** and **To** fields go one under the other below 520 px, each with its own label. See [Mobile quick guide](../../guida/mobile/#touch-targets) and [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#reading-the-register).
- **Latest changes in the HSE, Fitness and Tools views.** At the bottom of each of the three views, owners and verifiers find the collapsible **Latest changes** panel: the twenty most recent changes, with the action — created, updated, deleted —, author, date and time, record name and up to four changed fields. The panel is closed by default, loads when you open it and refreshes when you reopen it; when the rows reach twenty it warns that only the most recent are shown. See [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#latest-changes), [Fitness](../../moduli/idoneita/#latest-changes) and [Tools](../../moduli/attrezzature/#latest-changes).
- **An xlsx template to download for each export type.** In the **Import planning export** dialog the **Download template** button produces a file with the headers accepted for the chosen family and one example row: fill in the rows and delete the example row before importing. See [Importing a planning export](../../amministrazione/import-pianificazione/#preparing-the-sheet).

## 12 November 2026

- **Bigger controls on touch, indicators in two columns.** On a touch device buttons, small buttons, the **Board/List** selector and the hours booking stepper are at least **44 px** tall; on the desktop the sizes stay as before. Between **521 and 1100 px** the four Overview indicators move to two columns per row. See [Mobile quick guide](../../guida/mobile/#touch-targets) and [Common functions](../../guida/funzioni-comuni/#reading-the-overview).
- **The operational day rule stays on the phone.** In the Diary the **One operational day, two shifts** panel, which used to disappear below 800 px, is now a collapsible panel open by default: the rule and the contribution limit stay readable on a phone too. See [Site diary](../../moduli/diario/#the-operational-day).

- **Reconciliation without a status comparison.** The **To reconcile** panel in Deviations and the **Inconsistent timelines** section of the report check official dates only: closed without a date and closure before approval. The **Conflicting statuses** check — always zero, because deviations have no internal history — has been retired, together with the **Last internal status** column. See [Deviations](../../moduli/deviazioni/#to-reconcile).

- **Change history on HSE, Fitness and Tools.** Deleting an HSE event, a certificate or a tool no longer erases the row: it leaves the views, the counters and the details but stays in an audit register with every creation, edit and deletion, the author and the date. The register is readable through the API, with `GET /api/audit-events`, by owners and verifiers, and since 13 November also from the **Latest changes** panel of the three views. ## How the update works

1. Every change on `main` touching `app/`, `server/` or `database/` is detected.
2. The affected pages are reviewed and updated, in Italian and English.
3. The build checks the internal links; if something is broken, publishing does not pass.
4. The documentation change is a separate commit, labelled `docs(auto)`.

If a change requires screenshots or clarifications that the process cannot produce, a `docs-stale` issue is opened instead of inventing content.
