This page collects the changes visible to those who use the application. It is not a technical changelog: for that, see the commit history in the repository.

## 14 November 2026

- **Every dropdown is easy to tap.** On a touch device all dropdown menus are now at least **44 px** tall, not only the site selector: filters, sorting, forms and settings. Nothing changes on the desktop. See [Mobile quick guide](../../guida/mobile/#touch-targets).
- **Visual audit clean-up.** Three behaviour changes and a tidy-up. **Settings** no longer offer a **Decimals** choice: amounts always have two decimals, hours one decimal, counts none, and the chosen **Separators** apply to every number, not only to amounts. In **Deviations** the **To reconcile** panel on a phone moves **below** the board or the list, so filters and deviations stay at the top. In **HSE** the **Edit** and **Delete** commands of each row stay on one line, with a tap area of at least 44 px. The “Date: day/month/year” hint under date fields is gone: the field shows the date in the browser language while the saved value stays in ISO format. Small text — badges, notes, table headings — is a little larger. See [Settings](../../guida/impostazioni/), [Deviations](../../moduli/deviazioni/#to-reconcile) and [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#reading-the-register).
- **The deviations board in four groups.** The columns per single status become four groups: **Open** (`open` and `approved`), **In progress**, **To close** (work completed) and **Closed** (`closed` and `rejected`). Every card still shows its exact official status, and groups with an unrecognised status stay apart under **Unmapped status**. When the board is wider than the window the hint **scroll ↔** appears, with the edge faded on the side where more content is available. See [Deviations](../../moduli/deviazioni/#consulting-deviations).
- **Priorities in the interface language.** The priorities imported with deviations — `low`, `medium`, `normal`, `high`, `urgent` — now appear as **Low**, **Medium**, **Normal**, **High**, **Urgent** on cards, in the list, in the detail and in the **Priority** filter, and switch to Bassa, Media, Normale, Alta, Urgente with the **IT** selector. A value not in the list stays as it is in the imported data. See [Deviations](../../moduli/deviazioni/#consulting-deviations).
- **Light tasks as cards on a phone.** Below 800 px wide the **Light tasks** table becomes one card per task, with the label beside every field and the **Close** or **Reopen** command at full width, at least 44 px tall. Data, order and review rules do not change. See [Tasks and reviews](../../moduli/attivita/#light-tasks) and [Mobile quick guide](../../guida/mobile/#cards-instead-of-tables).

## 13 November 2026

- **Attachments on HSE events, with a read trail.** A saved event accepts photos or PDFs up to 15 MiB from the **Attachments** section of the edit window. The rule is “photograph the condition, not the people”: no identifiable faces and no health data, with a diagram or pictogram as an alternative to a photo; the application warns when the file name or description suggests a person or an injury, but it does not analyse the image and does not block the upload. Every opening of an HSE attachment writes a **Read** row in **Latest changes**, with the file name; deleting the event stops serving its attachments. See [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#attachments-on-an-event) and [REST API](../../riferimento/api/#attachments).

- **Four demo sites with different stories.** The demo environment now holds four sites — FITT-01, FITT-02, FITT-03 and FITT-04 — instead of two: a large one well advanced, a bridge just started, a small site only recently opened and one already finished. Each has its own **HSE** register and its own **Diary** entries, and whoever sees FITT-01 sees the others with the same role. These are test data: do not enter confidential information.
- **Collapsible side navigation and Today on the desktop.** On the desktop the side bar opens with the **Today** entry above the groups; each group — **Operations**, **Site**, **Management** — opens and closes when you click its heading, and the **▸** / **▾** arrow shows the state. The application remembers in that browser which groups you left open, opens by itself the group of the view you are opening and, on the first visit, keeps every group open only when the window is at least 800 px tall. See [Common features](../../guida/funzioni-comuni/#the-navigation-groups).
- **More readable secondary text, green focus ring.** Secondary text — subtitles, notes, table headings — has a higher contrast on the light background; the focus ring on buttons, links, fields and collapsible headings is now green with a white halo, instead of orange. See [Common features](../../guida/funzioni-comuni/).
- **Date fields with a hint, easier to tap.** Every date field — the diary's operational day, the HSE **From** / **To** filters and the dates in the input forms — shows the hint “Date: day/month/year” under the field, tied to the field for screen readers; the saved value stays in ISO format. On a touch device date fields are at least **44 px** tall and below **520 px** wide the field text is **16 px**, so the phone browser no longer zooms the page when you focus it. In the **HSE** view the **From** and **To** fields go one under the other below 520 px, each with its own label. See [Mobile quick guide](../../guida/mobile/#touch-targets) and [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#reading-the-register).
- **Latest changes in the HSE, Fitness and Tools views.** At the bottom of each of the three views, owners and verifiers find the collapsible **Latest changes** panel: the twenty most recent changes, with the action — created, updated, deleted —, author, date and time, record name and up to four changed fields. The panel is closed by default, loads when you open it and refreshes when you reopen it; when the rows reach twenty it warns that only the most recent are shown. See [HSE](../../moduli/hse/#latest-changes), [Fitness](../../moduli/idoneita/#latest-changes) and [Tools](../../moduli/attrezzature/#latest-changes).
- **An xlsx template to download for each export type.** In the **Import planning export** dialog the **Download template** button produces a file with the headers accepted for the chosen family and one example row: fill in the rows and delete the example row before importing. See [Importing a planning export](../../amministrazione/import-pianificazione/#preparing-the-sheet).

## 12 November 2026

- **Bigger controls on touch, indicators in two columns.** On a touch device buttons, small buttons, the **Board/List** selector and the hours booking stepper are at least **44 px** tall; on the desktop the sizes stay as before. Between **521 and 1100 px** the four Overview indicators move to two columns per row. See [Mobile quick guide](../../guida/mobile/#touch-targets) and [Common functions](../../guida/funzioni-comuni/#reading-the-overview).
- **The operational day rule stays on the phone.** In the Diary the **One operational day, two shifts** panel, which used to disappear below 800 px, is now a collapsible panel open by default: the rule and the contribution limit stay readable on a phone too. See [Site diary](../../moduli/diario/#the-operational-day).

- **Reconciliation without a status comparison.** The **To reconcile** panel in Deviations and the **Inconsistent timelines** section of the report check official dates only: closed without a date and closure before approval. The **Conflicting statuses** check — always zero, because deviations have no internal history — has been retired, together with the **Last internal status** column. See [Deviations](../../moduli/deviazioni/#to-reconcile).

- **Change history on HSE, Fitness and Tools.** Deleting an HSE event, a certificate or a tool no longer erases the row: it leaves the views, the counters and the details but stays in an audit register with every creation, edit and deletion, the author and the date. The register is readable through the API, with `GET /api/audit-events`, by owners and verifiers, and since 13 November also from the **Latest changes** panel of the three views. See [REST API](../../riferimento/api/#audit-register).

- **Today on a phone.** Below 800 px wide the first screen is **Today**, no longer the Overview: up to five rows with your assigned tasks that are due, the ones for you to verify, open steps, today's missing diary, open deviations and, at the bottom, overdue tasks with no executor, plus four **Quick actions** and the last three closures. Each row opens the view already filtered, with **Clear filters** at the top; the Overview moves to **More**. The desktop does not change. See [Mobile quick guide](../../guida/mobile/#the-today-screen).
- **People instead of names on tasks.** The **New task** form offers **executor** and **reviewer** chosen from the site members, with the role next to the name; **Another person…** keeps the free name for anyone without an account, such as a subcontractor. The member chosen as executor is disabled among reviewers, which show owners and the reviewer role only. A task linked to a person can be reviewed by that person; one with a name only stays with owners. See [Tasks and reviews](../../moduli/attivita/).
- **Planning export.** The button on the **Overview** and the import dialog now read **Import planning export**, with no third-party product name. The manual moves the page to [Import a planning export](../../amministrazione/import-pianificazione/) and the old addresses redirect there; file format, data families and preview do not change.

## 11 November 2026

- **To reconcile in deviations.** At the top of the **Deviations** view a collapsible panel reports deviations closed without a date, closed before approval and with conflicting statuses for at least three days; the buttons with the count filter the list and a row opens the detail. It is read only: no date is corrected. While the internal history is unavailable, statuses cannot be compared and the panel says so. The same checks appear in the report, in the **Inconsistent timelines** section. See [Deviations](../../moduli/deviazioni/#to-reconcile).
- **HSE register.** New **HSE** view, in the **Management** group: near misses, accidents and observations with date, type, severity, location, number of people involved and measures. It is a separate register, with no health data and no names: owners, editors and verifiers read it, owners and editors write it. See [HSE](../../moduli/hse/).
- **Light tasks.** In **Tasks** the third view shows ordinary tasks only in a bare table, with a single command per row — **Close** or **Reopen** — that follows the review rules and asks for a note or reason when needed. See [Tasks and reviews](../../moduli/attivita/).
- **Source hours and app hours.** **Time bookings** groups by day and employee and separates hours worked, hours booked from the source, hours booked in app and uncovered hours; the **variance** tells work without allocation apart from bookings beyond hours worked, and stays “not calculable” when a source total is missing. The Overview counts hours booked in app over the last six weeks. See [Time bookings](../../moduli/prenotazioni-ore/).

- **Personal Overview.** The **Customize** button chooses which blocks to show and in what order, per user and per site; **Restore defaults** returns to the full view. See [Common features](../../guida/funzioni-comuni/#reading-the-overview).
- **Module access.** An owner can exclude a member from a single module without changing their role, from the table at the bottom of the **Sites** view; excluded entries disappear from the navigation and the APIs answer `403`. See [Users and roles](../../amministrazione/utenti-e-ruoli/#module-access).
- **Planned hours on progress items.** The item detail has a **Planned hours** field, editable by owners and editors; when blank, it uses the hours of the linked WBS node when present. The hours feed earned value. See [Site progress](../../moduli/avanzamento/#planned-hours).
- **Earned value on the Overview and in the report.** New panel with EV, PV and AC in hours, CPI and SPI, calculated from planned hours and booked hours; when planned hours are missing the panel says so. See [Site progress](../../moduli/avanzamento/#site-report).
- **Column mapping in planning imports.** Each site can configure column aliases and row identity fields per family; the preview stays bound to the file and the mapping, and the defaults can be restored when needed. See [Import a planning export](../../amministrazione/import-pianificazione/#column-mapping).

- **Employee fitness.** New **Fitness** view, in the **Management** group of the navigation: qualifications, medical checks, training and other certificates, with issuer or doctor, issue date, expiry, days left and a status computed from the date — expired, expiring within 30 days, valid or no expiry. Owners and editors create, edit and delete; verifiers read; viewers have no access because these are personal records. See [Fitness](../../moduli/idoneita/).
- **Task type: ordinary or corrective.** The creation form has the **Type** field, the filter bar the **Type** menu, corrective cards the **Corrective** badge and the CSV export a **Type** column. See [Tasks and reviews](../../moduli/attivita/).
- **API limits and pages.** Requests to `/api/` are limited to 600 per minute per session and 60 per anonymous address, answering `429` with `Retry-After`; the tasks, diary, deviations, progress and bookings lists are read in pages with `limit` and `offset`, and the total arrives in the headers. See [REST API](../../riferimento/api/).
- **Tools register.** New **Tools** view, in the **Management** group of the navigation: name, code or serial number, contractor, status — available, in use, maintenance, retired — location and notes, with contractor and status filters. Owners, editors and verifiers add, edit and delete rows; viewers only read. See [Tools](../../moduli/attrezzature/).
- **Grouped desktop navigation.** On desktop the views are collected in **Operations**, **Site** and **Management**; the entries and the phone bottom bar do not change. See [Common features](../../guida/funzioni-comuni/#the-navigation-groups).
- **Buttons that match the roles.** The `viewer` role no longer sees **New task** on the Overview and in Tasks; **New site** appears only to owners. The server checks do not change. See [Users and roles](../../amministrazione/utenti-e-ruoli/).
- **Printable site report.** The **Print PDF** button on the Overview and in Site progress opens a report with two tables — progress by macro-area with actual, planned and the source of the value, and deviations by macro-area with total, open, closed or rejected and the count by status. Printing uses the browser, A4 and without buttons. See [Site progress](../../moduli/avanzamento/#site-report).
- **CSV export of the report.** Both report tables download as CSV files, with raw numbers and empty cells when a value is missing. See [Site progress](../../moduli/avanzamento/#site-report).
- **Saved views in Tasks.** Filters, board or list, sorting and visible columns are saved under a name; a view can be personal or shared with the site, and can be updated, renamed or deleted from the menu. See [Tasks and reviews](../../moduli/attivita/#saved-views).
- **WBS code and description in the progress detail.** The detail of a progress item shows the linked node as the code followed by the description, instead of the code alone. See [Site progress](../../moduli/avanzamento/#the-detail).

## 10 November 2026

- **Planning import from the Overview.** Owners and editors upload an xlsx file, see a preview with new, updated, unchanged, missing untouched and rejected rows, then confirm. No row is deleted. See [Import a planning export](../../amministrazione/import-pianificazione/).
- **Data assistant.** The **AI** button at the bottom right opens a panel with contextual suggestions and answers that cite records. It reads site data and writes nothing. See [Data assistant](../../guida/assistente-ai/).
- **Overview with counters and charts.** Four indicators (open deviations, booked hours, open claims, settled), a deviations-by-status donut, progress by section with a planned marker, and hours per week. See [Common features](../../guida/funzioni-comuni/).
- **Amount settings.** The gear at the top right sets currency, decimals and separators. See [Settings](../../guida/impostazioni/).
- **Photos and PDFs in tasks and diary.** Entries accept images and PDFs, including drag and drop from the desktop, up to 15 MiB per file. See [Site diary](../../moduli/diario/).
- **Sortable tables.** Module table headers sort the list; column filters are optional and tables become cards on phones. See [Common features](../../guida/funzioni-comuni/).
- **Mobile navigation.** On phones the bottom bar shows Overview, Tasks, Deviations, Hours and More. See [Mobile quick guide](../../guida/mobile/).
- **Claim status labels.** Cost impacts use their own labels — Open, Notification sent, Submitted, Closed — instead of the task ones. See [Cost impacts](../../moduli/impatti-economici/).
- **Diary writing for owners, editors and verifiers.** Users with the `viewer` role read the diary but do not see the button to add a contribution. See [Site diary](../../moduli/diario/).
- **Illustrated manual.** Procedures now have numbered steps, captioned images and what to do when something fails; the new [Mobile quick guide](../../guida/mobile/) covers the bottom bar, cards and touch targets.
- **Accordion boards on phones.** Tasks and Deviations show one status group at a time, opened with a tap, with no horizontal scrolling. See [Mobile quick guide](../../guida/mobile/).
- **Deviation detail tabs.** The detail separates **Data** and **References**; the project structure tree shows the progress percentage next to each item. See [Deviations](../../moduli/deviazioni/) and [Project structure](../../moduli/wbs/).
- **Clearer passwords.** The sign-in form has a button to show the password; the temporary-password notice can be dismissed with × for the browser session. See [Sign in and account](../../guida/accesso/).
- **Completion bar on sites.** Every site card shows the share of closed tasks. See [Sites](../../moduli/cantieri/).
- **Step weights and progress history.** Step weights must total 100; every recording stays in the history and feeds the curves. See [Site progress](../../moduli/avanzamento/).
- **Core module views.** Deviations, Site progress, Time bookings and Cost impacts have an interface view; imported data stays read-only, while step completion and hour booking are recorded in the application. See [Site progress](../../moduli/avanzamento/) and [REST API](../../riferimento/api/).
- **Site progress, deviations and cost impacts via API.** The endpoints return imported progress and deviations read-only; cost impacts are created via API and closed with a result also from the interface, linked to deviations of the same site. See [REST API](../../riferimento/api/).
- **Hours booked on attendance.** The hours of an attendance day can be assigned to a progress item; the total appears in the item detail. See [REST API](../../riferimento/api/).
- **Attachments with explicit limits.** Tasks and diary accept attachments from the interface; sites stay API only. Limit **15 MiB**, images or PDF only. See [REST API](../../riferimento/api/).

- **Self-service access request.** From the sign-in form you can press **Request access** and **Forgot password**; approval happens by an owner via email. See [Sign in and account](../../guida/accesso/).

- **Attendance via API.** Personnel attendance is available as a read-only projection via API, not yet in the interface. See [REST API](../../riferimento/api/).
- **Remodelled manual.** The documentation has been reorganised for those who use the application on site, with step-by-step guides per module. See [Product status](../rilascio/).

## How the update works

1. Every change on `main` touching `app/`, `server/` or `database/` is detected.
2. The affected pages are reviewed and updated, in Italian and English.
3. The build checks the internal links; if something is broken, publishing does not pass.
4. The documentation change is a separate commit, labelled `docs(auto)`.

If a change requires screenshots or clarifications that the process cannot produce, a `docs-stale` issue is opened instead of inventing content.
