SiteAssistant is organised into **views** that share the same context: the **selected site**. Change the site, and every view shows that site's data.

## The header

The header contains, from left to right:

- the **site selector**, which lists only the sites where you have a role;
- the **navigation** between views: **Overview**, **Sites**, **Tasks**, **Project structure**, **Deviations**, **Site progress**, **Time bookings**, **Cost impacts**, **Diary**;
- the gear for [Settings](../impostazioni/) (currency, decimals, separators);
- the link to the manual;
- the **language selector** (IT / EN);
- your name and the **Sign out** button.

The **AI** button at the bottom right opens the [data assistant](../assistente-ai/).

## Overview

It is the at-a-glance view of the selected site. At the top you find four operational and financial indicators:

- **Open deviations**: deviations not closed and not rejected;
- **Booked hours**: sum of the hours recorded on work days;
- **Open claims**: claimed amount of claims not closed;
- **Settled**: sum of the settlement amounts recorded on claims.

Below the indicators, four task counters: **To execute**, **To verify**, **Closed** and **Overdue**.

The Overview also shows three charts:

- **Deviations by status**: a donut with the number of deviations per status;
- **Progress by section**: bars with the effective progress and a marker for planned progress;
- **Hours per week**: bars of booked hours in the last six weeks present in the data.

The page closes with:

- the tasks to keep an eye on, ordered by urgency and due date;
- review progress: closed tasks out of the total, with the count of blocked and awaiting-review ones;
- the latest diary entry of the current operational day.

Owners and editors also find **Import planning export** here: the upload is described in [Import a planning export](../../amministrazione/import-pianificazione/).

## Tasks: board and list

The **Tasks** view has two representations with the same content:

- **Board:** columns by status, to see at a glance where each task stands.
- **List:** rows ordered by priority, due date and the people involved.

The available filters are free text, status, executor and priority. On narrow screens the board becomes a horizontal carousel.

## Tables

In the module views with a table — **Deviations**, **Site progress**, **Time bookings**, **Cost impacts** — the headers are clickable: the first click sorts ascending, the second descending. They also work from the keyboard with Enter or Space.

The **Column filters** button adds a row of fields above the table: each field filters its own column. Filters are off by default and do not appear on phones.

On phones tables become **cards**: each row is a card with a label and a value, with no horizontal scrolling.

## Language

The IT/EN selector translates the interface labels immediately. The content you enter — titles, notes, reasons, diary entries — stays in the language you wrote it in. The chosen language and the selected site are kept in memory until the page is reloaded.

## Exports and printing

- **CSV:** exports the tasks you are currently viewing with the active filters, not the whole archive.
- **Print diary:** uses the browser's print dialog; there is no server-generated PDF.

## Accessibility and devices

The interface is responsive and keyboard-navigable. The input forms are modal windows with focus management; validation errors are announced in the form's message area.

On phones navigation moves to a **bottom bar** with five items: **Overview**, **Tasks**, **Deviations**, **Hours** and **More**. **More** opens the list of the remaining views: Sites, Project structure, Site progress, Cost impacts and Diary.
