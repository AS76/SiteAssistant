The SiteAssistant demo uses **synthetic data only**. It contains no real people, companies, addresses or documents. Do not enter confidential information, credentials or contract terms.

## The sites

The demo holds the `FITT-01`…`FITT-05` demonstration sites and, since 19 November 2026, two pilot sites:

- **`NL-IT-01` — Northline Piacenza, intralogistics plant.** Italian data.
- **`NL-UK-01` — Northline Midlands, intralogistics installation.** English data.

They are twin sites of the same fictional programme: they exercise language separation, country modules and the portfolio view. Discipline content stays in the site's language; the interface follows the language chosen with the **IT/EN** selector.

## What they contain

The two pilot sites have tasks, diary, attendance, hours, progress, deviations, HSE, fitness, tools and logistics. The data spans several weeks, with different statuses and due dates, to exercise filters, counters and views.

**Not represented** in this version: the weekly plan with commitments and PPC, the document register with revisions, the photo archive by location and notifications. The pilot records them as friction; it does not pretend they exist.

## Switching site

The site selector is in the header. On a phone it is reachable from the top bar or the **More** entry. The choice applies to the site views; it does not change the interface language.

## Access

Pilot accounts are created by the administrator. Credentials are not published in the documentation. See [Sign in and account](../accesso/) to sign in and manage your password and passkeys.
