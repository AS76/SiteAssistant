**The demonstration sites contain synthetic operational records:** names of people and companies, documents and site records in the test datasets are fictional. Operating the **public demo service**, however, may involve processing real personal data associated with accounts, access requests, sessions and security logs. Do not enter real personal data, confidential information, credentials or contract terms into the demo. Processing by the public service must be described in the demo's own privacy notice, separately from this description of synthetic site data.

## Demo notice and limits

On your first visit, the **Demo data** notice explains that site data is fictional and that the dataset resets periodically. **Send feedback on GitHub** opens the project's issue tracker in a new tab. Use **×** to dismiss the notice. Your browser remembers the choice on later visits if it can store site data.

At the bottom of the page, **About the demo** opens the limits panel even after you dismiss the notice. Testers share the environment, and retention of data and changes is not guaranteed. The AI assistant uses fictional data, but requests count toward actual provider usage.

The panel lists four features outside the demo scope.

- Extended logistics
- Country-specific modules
- Quality and acceptance
- Location hierarchy

A demo visitor can upload attachments but cannot delete them: deletion stays reserved to the owner and editor roles, so that one tester cannot destroy what another uploaded in the same shared environment.

## The sites

The demo holds the `FITT-01`…`FITT-05` demonstration sites and, since 19 November 2026, two pilot sites:

- **`NL-IT-01` — Northline Piacenza, intralogistics plant.** Italian data.
- **`NL-UK-01` — Northline Midlands, intralogistics installation.** English data.

They are twin sites of the same fictional programme. Use them to try switching between sites with different data languages and to explore the portfolio view. Discipline content stays in the site's language; the interface follows the language chosen with the **IT/EN** selector.

## What they contain

The two pilot sites have tasks, diary, attendance, hours, progress, deviations, HSE, fitness, tools and logistics. The data spans several weeks, with different statuses and due dates, to exercise filters, counters and views.

**Not represented** in this version: the weekly plan with commitments and PPC, the document register with revisions, the photo archive by location and notifications. The pilot records them as friction; it does not pretend they exist.

## Switching site

The site selector is in the header. On a phone it is reachable from the top bar or the **More** entry. The choice applies to the site views; it does not change the interface language.

## Access

Pilot accounts are created by the administrator. Credentials are not published in the documentation. See [Sign in and account](../accesso/) to sign in and manage your password and passkeys.
