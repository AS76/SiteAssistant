On a phone SiteAssistant shows the **same data** and the **same permissions** as on the desktop. What changes is how you move around: the first screen is **Today**, which answers "what do I do now", navigation moves to the bottom, tables become cards and controls grow for your finger.

## The bottom bar

What it is for: reaching the four views you use most with your thumb, without opening a menu.

1. Open the application in your phone browser and sign in.
2. Use the **bottom bar**: **Today**, **Tasks**, **Deviations**, **Hours**, **More**.
3. Tap **Hours** for Time bookings: it is the same view the desktop calls *Time bookings*.
4. Tap **More** for the remaining views: **Overview**, **Sites**, **Project structure**, **Site progress**, **Cost impacts**, **Diary**, **Tools**, **Fitness**, **HSE**.
5. The active item is highlighted; when you are in a view opened from **More**, it is **More** that looks active.

Above the content the **header** stays: full-width site selector, then the settings gear, the manual link, the user name, **Sign out** and the **EN/IT** selector.

:::caution[If an item is missing]
- The bar has five items when you have access to every module: if your access excludes one, its entry does not appear and the bar shows fewer. An excluded module does not appear in **More** either.
- The bar appears only below **800 px** wide: on a landscape tablet you see the side navigation again.
- If **More** does not open the list, reload the page: the list is a window of the application, not a separate page.
:::

## The Today screen

What it is for: knowing what to do now without opening each module.

Below **800 px** wide the application opens on **Today** when the address does not name a view; on the desktop, and on a direct link to a view, the first screen stays the one you asked for. **Today** is always available and composes only the modules you can access.

The screen has three blocks:

1. **To do today**: at most five rows, without duplicates, in this order — your open tasks due today or already past (**Assigned to me**), the ones you have to verify (**For me to verify**), at most two progress items with open steps (**Open steps**), today's diary when it is missing (**Today’s diary is missing**), deviations not closed or rejected (**Read only**) and, at the bottom, overdue tasks with no executor (**no linked executor**). Each row also shows status and due date when there is one.
2. **Quick actions**: **Close task**, **Write diary**, **Take photo**, **Update progress**. They take at most two taps and follow your permissions: a command you cannot use is disabled, and an action from an excluded module does not appear.
3. **Recent actions**: the last three closed tasks, with their closing date; when the date is missing the row says so.

Tapping a row opens the target view **already filtered** on the record: **Tasks** in list mode, **Site progress** or **Deviations** in list mode, **Diary** on the given day. At the top of the view a note says the selection comes from **Today**, with **Clear filters** to drop it.

:::caution[If Today looks incomplete]
- **Site progress** and **Deviations** only show data already viewed in this session: the screen says so. Open their modules to refresh them.
- Tasks with no executor are not "yours": they appear at the bottom as **no linked executor** only when they were due before today.
- If the list is empty, the application says there are no actions to suggest from the available data: it does not mean the site has stopped.
- Offline the screen shows the latest data saved on the device and the number of queued writes, which sync when the network returns.
:::

## Today: what to do now

What it is for: opening the application and knowing where to start, without reading a dashboard.

On a phone the first screen is **Today** (on desktop it stays the Overview).

1. **To do today** — at most five rows, in order: tasks assigned to you that are due today or overdue, the ones you have to verify, up to two progress items with open steps, today's diary when missing, open deviations (read only). Tasks with no responsible are not *yours* and appear at the bottom, marked.
2. **Quick actions** — four commands with a verb: **Close a task**, **Write the diary**, **Take a photo**, **Update progress**. They land on the right view within one or two taps.
3. **Latest actions** — the three tasks closed most recently.
4. **Connection status** — offline, one line says how many changes are queued; the list stays the last loaded one and says so.

:::note[Why five rows]
The cap is deliberate: this is a decision screen, not a list. If you need more, the module view is one tap away.

## Cards instead of tables

What it is for: reading a table row without horizontal scrolling.

1. Open **Hours**, **Site progress** or **Cost impacts**; for **Deviations** choose **List**.
2. Each row becomes a **card**: the record title is at the top right, labels on the left and values on the right.
3. Scroll vertically to move from one card to the next.
4. Use the menus above the list to filter; on a phone the **Column filters** button does not open the field row.

- Long values wrap inside the card: you do not need to rotate the phone.
- If text leaves the screen, check the browser zoom: at 100% the card fills the available width.
- Totals at the top of a view (for example in **Cost impacts**) refer to the filtered data, not to the whole site.
:::

## Touch targets

What it is for: hitting the right control first time, even with gloves on.

1. The main buttons — **New task**, **Add contribution**, **Refresh data**, **Column filters** — have a large touch area and sit on a row separate from the content.
2. Dropdown menus are tall and full width: tap the menu and pick an item from the system list.
3. In the task and deviation boards, status groups are an **accordion**: tap a status heading to open or close it; one group is open at a time.
4. The **AI** button stays at the bottom right, above the content: open it for the assistant and close it with the panel's ×.

:::caution[If a tap does not do what you expect]
- In the board, a tap on a status heading opens or closes the group; a tap on a card opens the detail.
- If the **AI** button covers a control, scroll the page slightly: the button stays fixed at the bottom right and the control becomes reachable again.
- On very narrow screens the filter menus truncate their text: the selected value is what matters, not the label.
:::

:::caution[If a view does not load]
- **Deviations**, **Site progress**, **Time bookings** and **Cost impacts** require a connection: offline they show a message instead of data.
- **Today**, **Overview**, **Tasks** and **Diary** remain readable offline with the data saved on the device; queued changes sync when the network returns.
- If the data looks old, press **Refresh data** in the view or reload the page.
:::

## What does not change

- Sites, roles and permissions are the same as on the desktop; **Today** is one more view, not one more permission.
- The **IT/EN** selector and [Settings](../impostazioni/) stay in the header.
- CSV exports and diary printing use the phone browser's features.
