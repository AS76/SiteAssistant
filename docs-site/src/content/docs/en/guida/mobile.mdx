On a phone SiteAssistant shows the **same data** and the **same permissions** as on the desktop. What changes is how you move around: the first screen is **Today**, which answers "what do I do now", navigation moves to the bottom, tables become cards and controls grow for your finger.

## The bottom bar

What it is for: reaching the views you use most with your thumb, without opening a menu.

1. Open the application in your phone browser and sign in.
2. Use the **bottom bar**: **Today**, **Tasks**, **Diary**, **Deviations**, **Progress** and **More**.
3. **Today** is the first item and the opening screen; **Diary** and **Progress** are within thumb reach, not inside a menu.
4. Tap **More** for the remaining views: **Time bookings**, **Overview**, **Sites**, **Project structure**, **Cost impacts**, **Tools**, **Fitness**, **HSE**, plus **Site attendance** and **Procurement** if you have the respective permissions.
5. The active item is highlighted; when you are in a view opened from **More**, it is **More** that looks active.

Above the content the **header** stays: full-width site selector, then the **AI** button if the assistant is on for the selected site, the settings gear, the manual link, **Sign out** and the **EN/IT** selector. Below **640 px** the user name is hidden and the site selector takes a full row. The site name is not repeated in the page title, where only the code stays.

:::caution[If an item is missing]
- The bar has six items when you have access to every module: if your access excludes one, its entry does not appear and the bar shows fewer. An excluded module does not appear in **More** either.
- The bar appears only below **640 px** wide: on a landscape tablet you see the top navigation.
- If **More** does not open the list, reload the page: the list is a window of the application, not a separate page.
:::

## The Today screen

What it is for: knowing what to do now without opening each module.

Below **640 px** wide the application opens on **Today** when the address does not name a view; on the desktop, and on a direct link to a view, the first screen stays the one you asked for. **Today** is always available and composes only the modules you can access.

The screen is organised in sections:

1. **Now** highlights the first item to look at. When that item is a blocked task or a diary next step, it appears as a row you can tap.
2. **To verify** includes tasks awaiting review and tasks that were blocked during review.
3. **Today's work**: the tasks and progress items for the day.
4. **Upcoming**: what is due after the operational day, kept separate from current work.
5. **Quick actions**: **Verification list**, **Write diary**, **Take photo**, **Open progress**. They land on the right view within one or two taps and follow permissions: a command you cannot use is disabled.
6. **Latest actions**: the last three closed tasks, with their closing date.
7. **Connection status**: offline, one line says how many changes are queued.

- A **Blocked** row shows the title, the due date or **No due date**, and the most recent recorded **Reason**, if available. If the task is due on or before the operational day, the row also shows how many days overdue it is. A task due today shows **0 days overdue**. Tap the row to open the task details or **History** to see its status changes.
- The **Next step** row shows the text from the most recent **Diary** contribution for the selected site with a next step. If newer contributions leave the field blank, the row uses an earlier contribution. The row also shows its operational day and shift. Tap it to open the **Diary** on that day.
- Other rows open the corresponding view for the record, either a filtered list in **Tasks**, **Site progress** or **Deviations**, or the **Diary** on the given day. Use **Clear filters** to remove the selection from a list.

From **Now** or **To verify**, use **Verify** to confirm closure, or **Reject** to explain what needs redoing in the required note and return the task to **In progress**. After saving, you stay on **Today** and the list updates. You can act before the due date. If a task awaiting review is visible to you and you cannot verify or reject it, the card shows a permissions message.

If a task was blocked while it was **To verify**, **Resume** returns it to that status immediately when the button is available. See [Tasks and verification](../../moduli/attivita/#the-review).

:::caution[If Today looks incomplete]
- **Site progress** and **Deviations** load independently. Placeholder rows appear while they load.
- If a refresh fails, **Today** shows a warning and keeps previously loaded items for the site visible when available. If access fails, items from that resource are hidden. **Retry** reloads only the affected resource.
- Tasks with no executor are not "yours": they appear at the bottom as **no linked executor** only when they were due before today.
- If the list is empty, the application says there are no actions to suggest from the available data: it does not mean the site has stopped.
- Offline the screen shows the latest data saved on the device and the number of queued writes, which sync when the network returns; for a resource with no saved data the screen says it is unavailable offline.
:::

## Today: what to do now

What it is for: opening the application and knowing where to start, without reading a dashboard.

On a phone the first screen is **Today** (on desktop it stays the Overview).

1. **Now** shows the first item and the action it supports. For a blocked task or a diary next step, tap the row itself. If there are no items, the screen offers the diary or the available views.
2. **To verify** — the other tasks awaiting review or blocked during review.
3. **Today's work** — the tasks and progress items for the day; **Upcoming** keeps separate what is due after the operational day.
4. **Quick actions** — **Verification list**, **Write diary**, **Take photo**, **Open progress**.
5. **Latest actions** — the three tasks closed most recently.
6. **Connection status** — offline, one line says how many changes are queued; the list stays the last loaded one and says so.

## Cards instead of tables

What it is for: reading a table row without horizontal scrolling.

1. Open **Hours**, **Site progress** or **Cost impacts**; for **Deviations** choose **List**, for **Tasks** choose **Light tasks**.
2. Each row becomes a **card**: the record title is at the top right, labels on the left and values on the right.
3. Scroll vertically to move from one card to the next.
4. Use the menus above the list to filter; on a phone the **Column filters** button does not open the field row.
5. In **Light tasks** the **Close** or **Reopen** command fills a whole row of the card, at least **44 px** tall: it is the only command and stays disabled when the status does not allow the transition.

:::caution[If a card looks cut off]
- Long values wrap inside the card: you do not need to rotate the phone.
- If text leaves the screen, the page stays at its designed scale: pinch zoom is blocked (see **Zoom stays put**), so horizontal scrolling is not the answer — report the view and the phone width.
- Totals at the top of a view (for example in **Cost impacts**) refer to the filtered data, not to the whole site.
:::

## Touch targets

What it is for: hitting the right control first time, even with gloves on.

1. The main buttons — **New task**, **Close shift**, **Refresh data**, **Column filters** — have a large touch area and sit on a row separate from the content. On a touch device controls are at least **44 px** tall: that covers buttons, small buttons, the **Board/List** selector, date fields, the **+** and **−** buttons of the hours booking stepper, **every dropdown menu** — filters, sorting, forms and settings — and the two controls you tap first, the **site selector** and the **Manual** link. Desktop sizes do not change.
2. Date fields are tapped like buttons: below **520 px** wide the text in the field is **16 px**, the size under which the phone browser no longer zooms the page when you focus the field. The field shows the date in the browser language, with no repeated hint under it.
3. Dropdown menus are tall and full width: tap the menu and pick an item from the system list.
4. In the task and deviation boards, status groups are an **accordion**: tap a status heading to open or close it; one group is open at a time.
5. The **AI** button stays at the bottom right, above the content, and the same command is in the header: both open the same panel, closed with the ×.

:::caution[If a tap does not do what you expect]
- In the board, a tap on a status heading opens or closes the group; a tap on a card opens the detail.
- If the **AI** button covers a control, scroll the page slightly: the button stays fixed at the bottom right and the control becomes reachable again.
- On very narrow screens the filter menus truncate their text: the selected value is what matters, not the label.
- In the **HSE** view the two period fields, **From** and **To**, go one under the other at full width below **520 px**, each with its own label: they no longer share a half row. In HSE cards **Edit** and **Delete** stay on the same line, separated by a dot, with a tap area of at least **44 px**.
- In **Deviations** the **To reconcile** panel is no longer at the top: below **520 px** it appears after the board or the list, so filters and deviations are reached straight away.
:::

## Zoom stays put

What it is for: keeping the page at its designed scale while you work one-handed or with gloves on.

The application **blocks two-finger zoom** and the browser's multi-touch gestures: pinching to zoom in or out does not change the page scale, which stays the one it is drawn at. Scrolling, dragging and tapping stay native to the phone, and turning the phone still adapts the page as before.

This is deliberate: the page stays at the scale its controls are sized for, so cards, date fields and full-width buttons fill the available width.

:::caution[If the page looks too big or too small]
- The scale cannot be changed with your fingers and does not depend on an application setting: it is the one designed for the phone width.
- Above **640 px** navigation returns to the top; above **1180 px** the fixed desktop rail appears.
- If a block leaves the screen, report the view and the phone model: it is a layout defect, not a zoom question.
:::

## The remaining views

**Overview**, **Sites**, **Cost impacts** and **Diary** keep the desktop structure, with controls stacked vertically. Open **More** to pick one.

In the **Diary**, the panel that sits on the right on the desktop — **One operational day, two shifts**, with the operational day rule and the contribution limit — is a collapsible panel on a phone, **open by default**: the rule stays readable without taking over the page, and a tap on its heading closes or reopens it.

:::caution[If a view does not load]
- **Deviations**, **Site progress**, **Time bookings** and **Cost impacts** require a connection: offline they show a message instead of data.
- **Today**, **Overview**, **Tasks** and **Diary** remain readable offline with the data saved on the device; queued changes sync when the network returns.
- If the data looks old, press **Refresh data** in the view or reload the page.
:::

## What does not change

- Sites, roles and permissions are the same as on the desktop; **Today** is one more view, not one more permission.
- The **IT/EN** selector and [Settings](../impostazioni/) stay in the header.
- CSV exports and diary printing use the phone browser's features.
