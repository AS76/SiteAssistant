The gear at the top right opens **Settings**. Preferences apply to this browser and only affect how amounts and numbers are written.

## Setting currency and format

What it is for: reading amounts with the currency and separators you use.

1. Press the **gear** at the top right.
2. Choose the **Currency**: EUR, USD, GBP or CHF.
3. Choose **Separators**: `1.234,56` (IT) or `1,234.56` (EN).
4. Press **Save** and wait for the **Settings saved** confirmation at the bottom.

## The two choices

| Setting | Values |
| :-- | :-- |
| Currency | EUR, USD, GBP, CHF |
| Separators | 1.234,56 (IT) or 1,234.56 (EN) |

The defaults are **EUR** and **IT** separators.

## How numbers are written

Precision is not a choice: it depends on the kind of number and stays the same across every view.

- **amounts** always have **two decimals**;
- **hours** have **one decimal**;
- **counts** — tasks, deviations, people, days — are whole numbers, without decimals;
- **percentages** show up to four decimals, without trailing zeros.

The separators you choose apply to all these numbers, not only to amounts: with IT separators thousands are separated by a dot and decimals by a comma, with EN separators it is the other way round.

## Where they apply

The settings apply to amounts:

- the **Open claims** and **Settled** counters in the Overview;
- the totals and details of **Cost impacts**.

The **separators** also apply to hours, percentages and counts: **Earned value** and the **site report**, **Site progress**, **Time bookings**, **Deviations** and the tables of the other views. The choice does not change values, dates or calculations.

## The currency does not convert

Changing the currency changes the symbol and the format, not the value. An amount of 1,000 euros shown in USD stays 1,000: no exchange rate is applied.

Example: with EUR and IT separators, the amount 1234.5 appears as `€1.234,50`; with EN separators it appears as `€1,234.50`.

## Where they are stored

Preferences stay in this browser, not on the account. On another browser or device the defaults come back. If you clear the site data, the defaults come back on this browser too.

:::caution[If amounts do not change]
- Settings apply after **Save**: without confirmation the previous format stays.
- If you changed browser or device, preferences do not follow you: set them again.
- If the symbol changes but the number does not, that is expected: the currency does not apply an exchange rate.
- If an amount shows **Not available**, the source did not provide it: it is not a formatting problem.
:::
