The **diary** tells the story of every shift: what was planned, what was actually done, which impediments came up and what is handed over to the next shift. It is a handover, not a formal record.

## Where it is

The **Diary** view shows the entries of the selected site for the chosen **operational day**. From here you open the **Add contribution** form.

## Recording a contribution

What it is for: leaving the next shift the plan, the results and what is still open.

1. Choose the **operational day** with the date field: under the field the hint “Date: day/month/year” says in which order the date is read, while the saved value stays in ISO format (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
2. Press **Add contribution**.
3. Choose the **shift**: day or night.
4. Fill in **shift plan**, **actual results**, **blockers** and **next step**. The text fields are optional, but a contribution without results tells very little.
5. Attach photos if you need them (see the next section).
6. Press **Save**. The contribution appears in the day with the time it was recorded.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Shift | Yes | `day` or `night` |
| Plan | No | What was planned for the shift |
| Actual results | No | What was actually executed |
| Blockers | No | Impediments, interferences, variances |
| Next step | No | What is handed over to the next shift |

The operational day is computed by the application from the site and the time. There is **one entry per site, day and shift**: a second entry on the same combination is rejected.

Recording a contribution is reserved to **owners**, **editors** and **verifiers**. Users with the `viewer` role read the diary but do not see the **Add contribution** button.

:::caution[If the contribution is not saved]
- **The Add contribution button is missing:** your role is `viewer`. Ask an owner to record the contribution or to change your role on the site.
- **Duplicate contribution:** an entry already exists for that day and shift. Choose the other shift or open the existing entry.
- **Wrong day:** the date field decides the operational day; a night shift belongs to the day it started on.
- **Saving offline:** the contribution stays queued on the device and is sent when the network returns; the status is visible next to the entry.
:::

## Adding photos to the diary

What it is for: attaching to the shift the images that document the work and the PDFs that go with it.

1. In the **Add contribution** form, find the **Photos or documents** box.
2. Drag the files from the desktop into the box, or tap the box and pick files from the device.
3. You can select several files: images or PDFs, up to **15 MiB** each.
4. Check the count of selected files under the box.
5. Press **Save**: the entry is recorded and the files are attached right after.
6. On an entry already saved, use **Add photo or PDF** to add more attachments.

Uploading attachments is reserved to **owners** and **editors**.

:::caution[If photos are not attached]
- **You are offline:** the entry is saved anyway, but the files are not sent. The application says “Photos are attached with an active connection: the diary is saved”. Attach the photos when you are back online.
- **File rejected:** it must be an image or a PDF, not empty and not over 15 MiB.
- **Upload rejected:** attachments are reserved to owners and editors. A verifier can save the entry, but the photos are rejected and the entry stays saved.
- **Removing an attachment:** removal is not available in the interface, only via API.
:::

## The operational day

The operational day does not start at midnight:

- it starts at the configured cutoff time, by default **06:00** in the **Europe/Rome** timezone;
- it ends at 05:59:59 of the following day;
- a night shift belongs to the day it started on.

Example: a night shift starting on 12 November at 22:00 and ending on the 13th at 06:00 is recorded on **12 November**.

The rule also sits on the diary page: on the desktop in the **One operational day, two shifts** panel to the right of the entries, on a phone in a collapsible panel open by default.

## Printing

The **Print diary** button uses the browser's print dialog. There is no server-generated PDF and no archive of prints.

## What is not available yet

- Editing or deleting an entry after saving.
- Linking a diary entry to a task.
- Removing an attachment from the interface: removal is available via API only.
- Closing or formally signing the diary: contributions stay open and are saved on the server.
