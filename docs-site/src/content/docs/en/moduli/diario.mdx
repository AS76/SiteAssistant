The **diary** tells the story of every shift: what was planned, what was actually done, which impediments came up and what is handed over to the next shift. It is a handover, not a formal record.

## Where it is

The **Diary** view shows the entries of the selected site for the chosen **operational day**. From here you open the **Close the shift** form.

## Recording a contribution

What it is for: leaving the next shift the plan, the results and what is still open.

1. Choose the **operational day** with the date field: the field shows it according to the browser language, while the saved value stays in ISO format (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Next to the field the date appears in full in the interface language, for example “10 Nov 2026”.
2. Press **Close the shift**.
3. If you are recording the current shift online, the form shows the date and shift. For another day or shift, or when working offline, choose **Day shift** or **Night shift** in the form.
4. Fill in **shift plan**, **actual results**, **blockers** and **next step**. The text fields are optional, but a contribution without results tells very little.
5. Attach photos if you need them (see [Adding photos to the diary](#adding-photos-to-the-diary)). If the selected day is more than seven days ago, also enter a **Reason** of at least 10 characters.
6. Press **Save**. For the current shift online, the server assigns the day and shift when it records the entry. If the operational day changes in the meantime, the Diary opens on the recorded date.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Shift | Yes | Automatic for the current shift online. Otherwise, choose it in the form. |
| Plan | No | What was planned for the shift |
| Actual results | No | What was actually executed |
| Blockers | No | Impediments, interferences, variances |
| Next step | No | What is handed over to the next shift |

There is **one entry per site, day and shift**: a second entry on the same combination is rejected.

**Owners**, **editors** and **verifiers** with Diary access can record a contribution dated today or on any of the previous seven days. Older dates require an **owner**, and the entry shows **Recorded late on** with the actual date and time of recording. Future dates are excluded. The limit uses calendar days in the site's time zone, starting at local midnight.

:::caution[If the contribution is not saved]
- **Missing Close the shift button.** Check your role, the selected date and whether both shifts already have contributions.
- **Duplicate contribution.** Choose the other shift if that is the one you need to record, or use **Correct** on the existing entry if you have permission.
- **Saving offline.** The contribution stays queued on the device with the selected date and shift. When the connection returns, the server applies the seven-day limit at the time of synchronisation. The status is visible next to the entry.
:::

## Correcting an entry and viewing its history

1. When online, select the entry's day and press **Correct**. You can correct your own contribution up to seven calendar days after its operational date in the site's time zone. An **owner** can correct any entry and must enter a **Reason** of at least 10 characters for entries by other authors, entries without a linked author and their own entries older than seven days.
2. Update the **Shift plan**, **Actual results**, **Blockers / variances** or **Next step**. You can also clear a field. The operational day and shift stay as recorded.
3. Press **Save**. The entry shows the updated text, along with when the last correction was made and who made it.

**Version history** appears after the first correction and is available online to anyone with Diary access. Each version shows the content before the correction, who made the correction, when they made it and any reason they entered. The most recent corrections appear first.

## Adding photos to the diary

What it is for: attaching to the shift the images that document the work and the PDFs that go with it.

1. In the **Close the shift** form, find the **Photos or documents** box.
2. Drag the files from the desktop into the box, or tap the box and pick files from the device.
3. You can select several files: images or PDFs, up to **15 MiB** each.
4. Check the count of selected files under the box.
5. Press **Save**: the entry is recorded and the files are attached right after.
6. On an entry already saved, use **Add photo or PDF** to add more attachments.

Uploading attachments is reserved to **owners** and **editors**.

:::caution[If photos are not attached]
- **You are offline:** the entry is saved anyway, but the files are not sent. The application says “Photos are attached with an active connection: the diary is saved”. Attach the photos when you are back online.
- **File rejected:** it must be an image or a PDF, not empty and not over 15 MiB.
- **Upload rejected:** attachments are reserved to owners and editors. A verifier can save the entry, but the photos are rejected and the entry stays saved.
- **Removing an attachment:** removal is not available in the interface, only via API.
:::

## The operational day

The operational day begins at the site's local cutoff time and lasts until the next cutoff. Before that time, the previous operational day still applies. With the default cutoff of **06:00**, the day shift runs from 06:00 to 18:00 and the night shift from 18:00 to 06:00. The night shift boundary is fixed at 18:00.

For example, with a 06:00 cutoff, a contribution recorded at 02:00 on 13 November belongs to the night shift of **12 November**. The Diary displays the recording time in the time zone saved with that entry. See [Site settings](../../guida/impostazioni/#site-settings).

The rule also sits on the diary page, below the entries, in the collapsible **One operational day, two shifts** panel.

## Correcting an entry

Open **Correct** on a saved entry while online and enter a reason of at least 10 characters. An editor can correct entries for the current operational day of the site until the next cutoff, including after midnight if that day is still in progress. An owner can correct entries at any time.

**Version history** keeps the previous content of each correction, who made it, when and why. The recorded operational date stays fixed.

## Printing

The **Print diary** button uses the browser's print dialog. There is no server-generated PDF and no archive of prints.

## What is not available yet

- Deleting an entry after saving.
- Linking a diary entry to a task.
- Removing an attachment from the interface: removal is available via API only.
- Closing or formally signing the diary: **Close the shift** records a contribution, not a signature; contributions stay open and are saved on the server.
