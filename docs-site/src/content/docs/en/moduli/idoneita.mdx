---
title: Fitness
description: Employee qualifications, medical checks and training, with expiry and days left.
---

The **Fitness** view collects the certifications of the employees on the selected site: qualifications, medical checks, training courses and other certificates, each with issuer or doctor, issue date, expiry and outcome.

Unlike the data that comes from the site systems, certifications are created and maintained in the application. The status is not a stored field: it is computed from the expiry date every time the list is read, so there is nothing to re-align.

## Reading the certifications

What it is for: knowing who is fit, with which certificate and until when.

1. Open **Fitness** from the navigation, in the **Management** group.
2. Read the four counters at the top: **Expired**, **Expiring**, **Valid** and **No expiry**.
3. Narrow the list with the **Employee**, **Category** and **Status** menus.
4. Read the table: **Employee**, **Category**, **Certificate**, **Issuer or doctor**, **Issued**, **Expires**, **Days left** and **Status**.
5. Click a header to sort: the first click sorts ascending, the second descending.
6. Clear the three menus to go back to the full list.

The list starts sorted by expiry; certificates without an expiry date go last. The four counters count every certification on the site, not only those that pass the filters. In the **Days left** column an already expired certificate shows how many days ago it expired.

## The statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| :-- | :-- |
| Expired | The expiry date is in the past |
| Expiring | Expires within 30 days |
| Valid | Expires in more than 30 days |
| No expiry | No expiry date |

The **30 days** threshold is the default used by the interface. The calculation happens at read time: a certificate becomes **Expiring** and then **Expired** on its own, without anyone updating the row. For a different threshold see [REST API](../../riferimento/api/#fitness).

## Adding or editing a certificate

Owners and editors see the **New certificate** button.

1. Press **New certificate**.
2. Choose the **Employee** from the employees of the site.
3. Choose the **Category**: **Qualification**, **Medical check**, **Training** or **Other**.
4. Type the **Certificate**, required, from 1 to 120 characters.
5. Add **Issuer or doctor** (up to 200 characters), **Issued**, **Expires**, **Outcome** (up to 500 characters) and **Notes** (up to 2000 characters) if you need them.
6. Press **Save certificate**. The window closes, the counters and the list refresh and the message “Certificate saved.” appears.

To correct a row, press **Edit** on the row itself: the same window opens with the current values and the button becomes **Save changes**.

The **expiry** date cannot precede the **issue** date: if the two dates are swapped the save is rejected. A certificate without an expiry date stays **No expiry** and raises no warnings.

## Deleting a certificate

1. Press **Delete** on the row.
2. Check the name, employee and expiry shown in the confirmation.
3. Confirm with **Delete**.

The certificate disappears from the list and the counters, and later reads and edits on the same row return “not found”. The row itself is not erased: it stays in the audit register, which keeps a copy of the certificate as it was plus a record of every creation, edit and deletion, with author and date. The trail is readable from the **Latest changes** panel in the view and through the API, by owners and verifiers: see [Latest changes](#latest-changes) and [Audit register](../../riferimento/api/#audit-register). If the certificate must stay readable in the interface, correct its data instead of deleting it.

## Latest changes

At the bottom of the view, owners and verifiers have the collapsible **Latest changes** panel: the twenty most recent changes to the site certificates, newest first. It is closed by default and the list loads when you open it.

1. Each row gives the action — **Created**, **Updated** or **Deleted** —, who made it and when.
2. Below it the certificate name; if the row does not carry one, the first eight characters of the identifier.
3. For an edit, up to four changed fields appear as **field: before → after**; when more fields changed, the row says how many others remain.
4. When the list reaches twenty rows the panel warns that only the most recent changes are shown: to go further back use `GET /api/audit-events` with `limit` and `offset`.

Values are the stored ones: for the category you see the code, not the translated label, and dates stay in `YYYY-MM-DD` format. The panel reloads every time you open it, so close and reopen it to see changes just made. Deleted certificates stay readable here, with the data they had before deletion. Editors and viewers do not see the panel.

## Who can do what

| Action | Role |
| :-- | :-- |
| Read the certifications | owner, editor, verifier |
| Add, edit or delete a certificate | owner, editor |
| Read the latest changes | owner, verifier |

Certifications are personal records: the `viewer` role does not read them. The view appears in the navigation, but for a viewer the server refuses to load the list.

:::caution[If something does not add up]
- **I cannot see the data, or an error appears:** your role on the site is `viewer`; reading is reserved to owners, editors and verifiers. See [Users and roles](../../amministrazione/utenti-e-ruoli/).
- **I cannot see the buttons:** only owners and editors write; verifiers only read.
- **The employee is not in the list:** the menu offers the employees of the site; a person who is not among the site attendance records cannot be selected.
- **“This certificate is no longer available”:** someone else deleted it. Close the window and reload the list.
- **The counters do not follow the filters:** that is intentional; they count every certification on the site.
- **Offline:** the view requires a connection, like the other module views; without a network you can neither read nor edit certifications.
- **Empty list:** the site has no certifications yet, or the three filters add up; clear employee, category and status.
:::

The same rows are available through the API, with the `/api/certifications` endpoints: see [REST API](../../riferimento/api/#fitness).
