The **Deviations** view shows the official data imported for the selected site. Open a deviation to record its internal owner, attribution and registry links. For totals per PLC area and the count by status, see the [site report](../avanzamento/#site-report).

## Consulting deviations

What it is for: knowing which deviations are open, who follows them and what has already been closed.

1. Open **Deviations** from the navigation.
2. Filter by **Status** and **Priority**. The menus include every value present in the data, plus “All statuses” and “All priorities”.
3. Choose the representation: **Board** for the groups by status, **List** for the table.
4. Open a card or a row for the detail.
5. In the detail use the **Data** and **References** tabs: the first gathers status, priority, description and amounts, the second the links.

The **board** groups deviations into four status groups:

- **Open** — status `open` or `approved`;
- **In progress** — status `in_progress`;
- **To close** — work completed, status `work_completed`;
- **Closed** — status `closed` or `rejected`.

The grouping is presentational only: every card still shows its exact official status, for example **Approved** inside **Open** or **Rejected** inside **Closed**. Each group shows how many deviations it holds; an empty group says “No deviations”.

Deviations with an unrecognised status stay outside the four groups and appear separately, under **Unmapped status**. When the board is wider than the window, the hint **scroll ↔** appears below it and the edge fades on the side where more content is available; the hint disappears once every group is visible. On a phone the groups are an accordion, one open at a time.

**Priority** also appears with its translated label in the interface language — **Low**, **Medium**, **Normal**, **High**, **Urgent** — on cards, in the list, in the detail and in the **Priority** filter; a value not in the list stays as it is in the imported data. Switching to IT changes the cards and menus together.

In the **list** the columns are reference, title, status, priority, assignee, created and closed; headers sort the table.

:::caution[If a deviation is missing]
- Check the **Status** and **Priority** filters: an active filter narrows the list without warning.
- Deviations come from the planning import: a deviation missing from the file does not exist on the site. It cannot be created by hand from this view.
- If the status is not one of the expected ones, the card appears under **Unmapped status**: the export needs fixing.
- The view requires a connection: offline it shows a message instead of the data.
:::

## To reconcile

What it is for: seeing where a deviation's dates disagree with each other, without correcting them.

At the top of the view, the collapsible **To reconcile** panel gathers the date checks on the site's deviations (on a phone, below **520 px**, the panel moves **below** the board or the list, so filters and deviations stay at the top):

- **Closed without date** — the status is `closed` but the closure date is missing.
- **Closure before approval** — the closure date comes before the approval date; the **days apart** are the difference between the two dates.

The panel is **read only**: no date is corrected. The two buttons with the count filter the table; a second click removes the filter. The table shows reference, title, official status, days apart and reason, and a click on a row opens the deviation detail. When there is nothing to report, “Timelines aligned” appears.

The panel compares official dates. The timeline in the detail records import events and internal decisions.

The same checks appear in the [site report](../avanzamento/#site-report), in the **Inconsistent timelines** section.

## The detail

The detail is split into two tabs:

- **Data:** reference, status and priority; problem description, author and assignee; PLC area and creation, due, approval and closure dates; actual cost, claimed quantity, labour hours and completed task percentage.
- **References:** the **linked references** — progress item, task or diary entry.

Switch tabs with a click or with Tab and Enter. To correct an official field, update the planning export and import it again.

:::caution[If the detail does not open]
- **Record no longer available:** the data changed after loading. Close the detail and reload the view.
- **Reference without a detail:** for tasks, diary and project structure the reference stays a label, not a link.
- **Offline:** the deviation detail requires a connection, even if the list was already visible.
:::

## Link a site registry record

Under **Data → Site registry links**, open the field you want to reconcile. The **author** can link to a person. The **assignee** can link to a person or team. The **causing company** and **resolving company** can each link to a company.

1. The owner activates deviation links for the site. The preview shows the original text and either the recorded import source or the internal attribution state, depending on the field.
2. Select an active record on the same site and enter the reason and the evidence you used to identify it. An identical name is only a suggestion. Choose **Leave unresolved** if the case is ambiguous, the imported identity is unresolved, the field is blank or the attribution is disputed.
3. Select **Approve decision**, then **Apply batch**. The detail retains the original text and the record’s type, name and revision at approval. Archived records remain visible in history. Search returns active records.

Internal attribution has its own states **Undetermined**, **Agreed** and **Disputed**. Linking a company identifies the registry record the text refers to. Change the attribution state in the **Attribution** form. Registry links grant no site access, operational authority or team membership.

Open a new preview when the source data for the field changes. The previous snapshot remains available in history. The owner can roll back a batch by entering a reason. Rollback disables deviation links for the site and closes intervals still associated with that batch. Manual attribution corrections and later links stay on record.

## Who can do what

| Action | Role |
| :-- | :-- |
| View list, board and detail | Every site member |
| View registry previews, decisions and snapshots | Owners, editors, verifiers and viewers with access to Deviations and Registry |
| Approve and apply links | Owners and editors with access to both modules |
| Activate links or roll back a batch | Owners |
