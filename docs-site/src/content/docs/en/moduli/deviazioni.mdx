The **Deviations** view shows the deviations of the selected site. They are data **imported from the planning system** and are read-only here: you do not create or edit them in the application. For totals per PLC area and the count by status in a printable table, see the [site report](../avanzamento/#site-report).

## Consulting deviations

What it is for: knowing which deviations are open, who follows them and what has already been closed.

1. Open **Deviations** from the navigation.
2. Filter by **Status** and **Priority**. The menus include every value present in the data, plus “All statuses” and “All priorities”.
3. Choose the representation: **Board** for the columns by status, **List** for the table.
4. Open a card or a row for the detail.
5. In the detail use the **Data** and **References** tabs: the first gathers status, priority, description and amounts, the second the links.

In the **board** each column shows the number of deviations; an empty column says “No deviations”. Deviations with an unrecognised status end up in a separate **Unmapped status** section.

In the **list** the columns are reference, title, status, priority, assignee, created and closed; headers sort the table.

:::caution[If a deviation is missing]
- Check the **Status** and **Priority** filters: an active filter narrows the list without warning.
- Deviations come from the planning import: a deviation missing from the file does not exist on the site. It cannot be created by hand from this view.
- If the status is not one of the expected ones, the card appears under **Unmapped status**: the export needs fixing.
- The view requires a connection: offline it shows a message instead of the data.
:::

## To reconcile

What it is for: seeing where a deviation's dates and statuses disagree, without correcting them.

At the top of the view, the collapsible **To reconcile** panel gathers the checks on the site's deviations:

- **Closed without date** — the status is `closed` but the closure date is missing.
- **Closure before approval** — the closure date comes before the approval date; the **days apart** are the difference between the two dates.
- **Conflicting statuses (≥ 3 days)** — the official status differs from the internal one and the distance between the last internal update and the official date of the status is at least three days.

The panel is **read only**: no date is corrected. The three buttons with the count filter the table; a second click removes the filter. The table shows reference, title, official status, last internal status, days apart and reason, and a click on a row opens the deviation detail. When there is nothing to report, “Timelines aligned for the checks that can be evaluated” appears.

:::note[Conflicting statuses cannot be evaluated today]
The internal history of deviations is unavailable: deviations are only imported, so a status disagreement is not reported and the **Conflicting statuses** count stays at zero without confirming alignment. Official dates are still checked. The panel says so in a note.
:::

The same checks appear in the [site report](../avanzamento/#site-report), in the **Inconsistent timelines** section.

## The detail

The detail is split into two tabs:

- **Data:** reference, status and priority; problem description, author and assignee; PLC area and creation, due, approval and closure dates; actual cost, claimed quantity, labour hours and completed task percentage.
- **References:** the **linked references** — progress item, task or diary entry.

Switch tabs with a click or with Tab and Enter. The fields are read-only. To change a deviation, change the planning export and repeat the import.

:::caution[If the detail does not open]
- **Record no longer available:** the data changed after loading. Close the detail and reload the view.
- **Reference without a detail:** for tasks, diary and project structure the reference stays a label, not a link.
- **Offline:** the deviation detail requires a connection, even if the list was already visible.
:::
