The **Time bookings** view collects the hours recorded on the site's work days, grouped by **operational day and employee**. Work days and progress items come from the planning import; hours are booked here.

## Source hours and app hours

What it is for: telling apart the hours that come from the export from those entered in SiteAssistant, without mixing them up.

Every group has a summary row with five values:

- **Hours worked (source)**: the clocked hours of the imported work day;
- **Hours booked (source)**: hours already booked in the planning export;
- **Hours booked in app**: bookings entered in SiteAssistant, editable;
- **Uncovered hours (source)**: hours worked that the export does not cover with a booking; this is the export snapshot and does not indicate today's availability;
- **Variance**: **hours worked − (source booked hours + app booked hours)**.

Read the variance like this: **positive** means work without allocation, **negative** means booked beyond hours worked, **zero** means worked and booked hours match.

If a source total is missing — even on a single work day of the group — the variance is **not calculable**. The application does not treat a missing value as zero: an incomplete summary stays incomplete and says so.

## Booking hours in the app

What it is for: attributing the hours an employee worked on a given day to a progress item.

1. Open **Time bookings**. On a phone the item is called **Hours**.
2. In the **Book hours in app** form, choose the **work day / attendance**: the list shows date, employee, shift and start time.
3. Choose the **progress item** to charge the hours to.
4. Enter the **hours booked in app**. The field accepts half hours; the **−0.5 hours**, **+0.5 hours** and **+1 hour** buttons adjust the value without typing.
5. Press **Book hours in app**. The booking appears in the day and employee group, and the summary refreshes.

The **Operational day** menu filters the list to a single day; **All operational days** shows the groups from the most recent date. The list loads every booking of the site, not just the first page.

:::caution[If you cannot book]
- **No form at the top of the page:** your role is `verifier` or `viewer`, or the prerequisites are missing. Booking hours is reserved to **owners** and **editors**.
- **Message about prerequisites:** imported work days and progress items are required. An administrator must complete the import.
- **Duplicate booking:** a booking already exists for that work day and that same item. Change the hours on the existing booking instead of creating a second one.
- **Work day and item from different sites:** pick a work day and an item from the same site.
:::

## Reading and correcting

What it is for: comparing the app hours with the source and fixing a wrong booking.

1. Find the group: the heading shows **date · employee**.
2. Read the summary row: worked, source booked, app booked, uncovered and variance.
3. Compare the rows below the summary: employee, progress item and hours booked in app.
4. To delete a booking, press **Delete** on the row.
5. Confirm in **Delete booking**. The operation cannot be undone from the interface.

The summary counts each work day of the group once, even when a work day has several bookings. A group can appear with no rows: the work day exists, but no hour is allocated yet.

:::caution[If totals do not add up]
- **The variance is per group.** It is calculated for that date and employee; for the site picture use **All operational days** and compare the groups.
- **“Not calculable” is not zero.** It means at least one source total is missing: check the work day import.
- **Uncovered hours are not availability.** They are the export snapshot: a work day can look uncovered even if hours were booked after the import.
- **If the list is empty**, the site has no work days or progress items yet: it is not a filter problem.
- **Hours are booked against the imported work day**: a booking cannot be created without one.
:::

## Overview

The Overview counts the **hours booked in app over the last six weeks**, and the hours-by-week chart places, for each week, the app booked hours beside the source hours worked. The two measures stay separate: the first is editable from the application, the second is the export's.
