The **Time bookings** view collects the hours recorded on the site's work days, grouped by **operational day**. Work days and progress items come from the planning import; hours are recorded here.

## Booking hours

What it is for: attributing the hours an employee worked on a given day to a progress item.

1. Open **Time bookings**. On a phone the item is called **Hours**.
2. In the **Book hours** form, choose the **work day / attendance**: the list shows date, employee, shift and start time.
3. Choose the **progress item** to charge the hours to.
4. Enter the **hours**. The field accepts half hours; the **−0.5 hours**, **+0.5 hours** and **+1 hour** buttons adjust the value without typing.
5. Press **Book hours**. The booking appears in the day group, with the updated total.

The **Operational day** menu filters the list to a single day; **All operational days** shows the groups in descending date order.

:::caution[If you cannot book]
- **No form at the top of the page:** your role is `verifier` or `viewer`, or the prerequisites are missing. Booking hours is reserved to **owners** and **editors**.
- **Message about prerequisites:** imported work days and progress items are required. An administrator must complete the import.
- **Duplicate booking:** a booking already exists for that work day and that same item. Change the hours on the existing booking instead of creating a second one.
- **Work day and item from different sites:** pick a work day and an item from the same site.
:::

## Reading and correcting

What it is for: checking the day's hours and fixing a wrong booking.

1. Find the **operational day** group: the heading shows the date and the total hours.
2. Compare the rows with the day's work days: employee, item and hours.
3. To delete a booking, press **Delete** on the row.
4. Confirm in **Delete booking**. The operation cannot be undone from the interface.

The hours of each group are the sum of the visible rows; the day filter does not change the row values.

:::caution[If totals do not add up]
- The total next to the date sums only that group's rows: use **All operational days** for the full picture.
- If the list is empty, the site has no work days or progress items yet: it is not a filter problem.
- Hours are recorded against the imported work day: a booking cannot be created without one.
:::

## Who can do what

| Action | Role |
| :-- | :-- |
| View bookings and totals | Every site member |
| Book hours | owner, editor |
| Delete a booking | owner, editor |

Work day and employee data come from the import: for the format see [Import a planning export](../../amministrazione/import-pianificazione/).
