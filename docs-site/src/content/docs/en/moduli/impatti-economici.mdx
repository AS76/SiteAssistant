The **Cost impacts** view collects the claims linked to deviations. It shows how much has been claimed, how much has been settled and the status of each claim.

## Reading the totals

What it is for: knowing the value of open claims and what has already been recognised.

1. Open **Cost impacts** from the navigation.
2. Read **Total claimed** and **Total settled** at the top.
3. Apply the **Status** and **Result** filters to narrow the list.
4. Read the totals again: they refer to the filtered data, not to the whole site.
5. Open a row for the claim detail and its linked deviations.

Below the totals, a note recalls that the currency is not specified by the source: symbol and format follow [Settings](../../guida/impostazioni/), the value does not. When an amount is missing, the total excludes it and says so.

:::caution[If a total looks wrong]
- **The total changes when you filter:** that is expected. Totals are calculated on the visible rows.
- **It says “unavailable amounts excluded from total”:** the source did not provide those amounts; they are not zero.
- **The currency is not the one you expect:** check the settings gear; changing currency does not convert amounts.
- **Empty list:** the Status and Result filters add up; clear one of them.
:::

## The detail and transitions

The detail shows status, claimed amount, settlement amount, result, creation and closure dates, and the **linked deviations** with their status.

Owners and verifiers also find the status transition:

1. Open the claim.
2. Choose the target status under **Move to**.
3. If you close the claim, choose the **result**: settled, dropped or rejected.
4. Press **Save**. The detail refreshes with the new status.

Available transitions depend on the current status: from open you move to notification sent or closed, from notification sent to submitted, from submitted to closed. A closed claim has no transitions.

The claim statuses are **Open**, **Notification sent**, **Submitted** and **Closed**; the results are **Settled**, **Dropped** and **Rejected**.

:::caution[If the transition is not saved]
- **Missing result on closing:** closing requires a result among settled, dropped and rejected.
- **“The claim has changed”:** someone else updated the record. Reopen the detail and repeat the transition.
- **Insufficient role:** transitions are reserved to owners and verifiers; editors only consult.
- **Offline:** the view and the transitions require a connection.
:::
