The **task** is the unit of work on site. Every task belongs to a site, has a distinct **executor** and **reviewer**, and only moves through allowed transitions. The golden rule: **whoever executes cannot review their own work**.

## Create a task

What it is for: putting work on site with someone who executes it and someone who verifies it.

1. Open **Tasks** and press **New task**.
2. Fill in title, area and company.
3. Choose **executor** and **reviewer** from the site member lists; for someone without an account, such as a subcontractor, choose **Another person…** and type the name. They must still be two different people.
4. Choose the **type** — **Ordinary** or **Corrective** — the **priority** and, if needed, the **due date**.
5. If the site has a **Project structure** (WBS), choose which node to link the task to; the default is **Unlinked**, and you can link it later from the card.
6. Press **Save**: the task starts in status `todo` and appears in the **To do** column.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Title | Yes | At most 180 characters |
| Area | No | At most 100 characters |
| Company | No | At most 100 characters |
| Executor | Yes | A site member chosen from the list, or a free name |
| Reviewer | Yes | The person who checks and closes the work; owners and the reviewer role only |
| Type | No | **Ordinary** (default) or **Corrective** |
| Priority | Yes | `normal`, `high`, `urgent` |
| Due date | No | Date, pre-filled with today |
| Project structure | No | A WBS node of the site; when left empty the card shows **Unlinked** |

**The executor and the reviewer must be two different people**: the application rejects the assignment if they coincide.

**Choosing a member links the task to their account.** The name is still written on the task as a reference, but the chosen person can review it. With **Another person…** the task carries only a name: it is not linked to an account and reviews stay with owners. The reviewer list shows owners and the reviewer role only, and the person already chosen as executor appears disabled.

:::caution[If the task is not created]
- **Executor and reviewer are the same person:** choose two different people; the rule applies to owners too.
- **Member list unavailable:** the form says so above the buttons. You can still choose **Another person…** and enter a free name.
- **Required fields empty:** the form shows the error above the buttons and moves focus to the first field to fix.
- **Task created but not visible:** check the board filters and the selected site.
:::

## Ordinary and corrective tasks

The **type** tells planned work apart from work that fixes something:

- **Ordinary**: the planned work. It is the default and it is not stored as a type, so tasks created before this classification stay ordinary.
- **Corrective**: work that fixes a defect or a non-conformity. The board cards show a **Corrective** badge.

The type is chosen at creation and cannot be changed afterwards: tasks are not editable. The **Type** filter in the filter bar applies to the board, to the list, to the **Light tasks** view and to the CSV export, which has a **Type** column with the translated label.

## The statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| :-- | :-- |
| `todo` | To do: planned, not started yet |
| `in_progress` | In progress |
| `to_verify` | Reported done, awaiting review |
| `closed` | Closed after verification, by waiver or in historical records |
| `blocked` | Stopped by an impediment |

## Move a task forward

What it is for: recording the status change with the person, the time and the note that justify it.

1. Open the task detail from the card or the row.
2. Look at the **current status** and the available buttons: they change with the status.
3. Press the button for the move you need — for example **Start**, **Submit for review**, **Block**.
4. Write the **note** or **reason** the move requires.
5. Confirm in the form when prompted. The task’s **History** records who changed the status, when and with which note.

| From | To | What you do | What it requires |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `todo` | `in_progress` | **Start** | — |
| `todo` | `blocked` | **Block** | A reason |
| `in_progress` | `to_verify` | **Submit for review** | An execution note |
| `in_progress` | `blocked` | **Block** | A reason |
| `to_verify` | `closed` | **Verify** | An attachment and a verification note, or a waiver with a reason |
| `to_verify` | `in_progress` | **Reject** | A required note explaining what to redo |
| `to_verify` | `blocked` | **Block** | A reason |
| `closed` | `in_progress` | **Reopen** | A reason; only the designated reviewer or an owner |
| `blocked` | Status before the block | **Resume** | Takes effect immediately if the previous status is available |

:::caution[If the move is not available]
- **No buttons in the detail:** the current status does not allow that move. Reopen the detail to refresh the data.
- **Missing note or reason:** the form rejects the save and points to the field.
- For a **To verify** task, the details show a message if your account cannot verify or reject it.
:::

## Change a status from the board

What it is for: making the most common status move without opening the detail.

1. On the board, look at the corner of a task card: the **⋯** button opens the **Change status** menu.
2. The dialog shows the current status and only the moves allowed from it, with buttons such as **Verify**, **Reject** and **Resume** when available.
3. Press the move you need: when it requires a note or a reason the usual dialog opens, otherwise the move is recorded straight away.
4. The menu follows the same rules as the detail: permissions, allowed transitions and history are the same. Those who cannot write do not see the **⋯** button.

For less frequent moves — or to read the history while you decide — open the detail as described above: both paths lead to the same result.

## Link a task to the WBS

What it is for: anchoring each task to the **Project structure** node it belongs to, so progress and planning speak about the same work line.

1. The link is also chosen at creation, with the **Project structure** selector in the **New task** form; if you leave it empty the card shows **Unlinked**.
2. Open the task detail: the **Project structure** row shows the linked node, with its code and description.
3. For owners and editors the same row offers **Link to WBS**: the list shows the site's nodes and preselects the current one.
4. Pick the node and press **Save**: cards, detail, filters and linked views show the new link immediately.

You can change the link later by reopening the same dialog. When the site has no imported structure the command does not appear: import or create the structure from the **Project structure** view (see [its module page](/en/moduli/wbs/)).

## The review

Closing requires two distinct acts:

1. the **executor** reports completion with an execution note;
2. the **reviewer** chooses **Verify** and confirms closure. The form requires a task attachment and a verification note, or a waiver reason of at least 15 characters.

If the work needs another pass, choose **Reject**, explain what must be redone in the required **Note**, and confirm with **Reject**. The task returns to **In progress** and the note appears in **History**. If saving fails, the dialog shows the error and keeps your note.

The reviewer linked to the task and site owners can **Verify** or **Reject** when the task has a recorded submitter other than themselves. If the reviewer was entered as a name only, these actions are reserved for owners.

## Attachments

In a task detail you can attach photos or PDFs, up to **15 MiB** per file; uploading is reserved to owners and editors. For closure requirements, see [The review](#the-review).

From the board and the list you open the detail with the fields and the full history. The filters (text, status, executor, priority, type) apply to all three views and to the CSV export.

## Progress at 100% with open verifications

If you have access to **Site progress**, **To verify** tasks show any progress items at 100% on the same WBS node, with a warning that counts all tasks awaiting verification on the node. The warning appears on the board, in the list and in **Light tasks**. Select the item name to open its detail. Offline, when the warning is available in the data saved on the device, the item name appears as text. Use the usual verification action to close the task. See [Site progress](../avanzamento/).

Verification closes the task; formal acceptance is a separate record with a document and a date, and it is not the criterion for the warning.

## Light tasks

What it is for: closing or reopening ordinary tasks that go through a review, without opening the detail.

1. Open **Tasks** and choose **Light tasks** next to **Board** and **List**.
2. Set the search and filters as in the other views: they apply here too.
3. Read the table: **Task**, **Area**, **Executor**, **Due date**, **Status** and **Action**.
4. Press **Close** on a task waiting for review, or **Reopen** on a closed one.
5. The button starts the transition: when it requires a note or a reason the usual window opens — closing asks for the verification note, reopening for the reason — and the transition is recorded on confirmation.

The view shows **ordinary** tasks only: corrective ones stay on the board and in the list. The order puts open tasks first and closed ones after; within each group, tasks with a due date come first, from the nearest, then those without a due date, then by title.

Closing is reserved to the assigned reviewer or an owner, and reopening requires a reason. The button stays disabled when the status does not allow the transition — for example a **To do** or **In progress** task cannot be closed with one click, it must be submitted for review first — and while a change on the same task is still queued or has failed. The `viewer` role sees a dash instead of the button.

On a phone, below 800 px wide, the table becomes one **card per task**: every field has its own label beside it and the **Close** or **Reopen** command fills the last row, at least 44 px tall. The data and the rules do not change, only the shape. See [Mobile quick guide](../../guida/mobile/#cards-instead-of-tables).

## Saved views

What it is for: getting back in one click the filters, sorting and columns you use every day.

1. Open **Tasks**: above the filters you find **Saved view**, with the entry **No view**.
2. Set the filters and choose **Board**, **List** or **Light tasks**; in the list also set **Sort by**, **Direction** and the **Visible columns**.
3. Press **Save as view**, give it a name (at most 80 characters) and, if you want, select **Share with site members**.
4. Pick the view from the menu to apply it again; the selection applies to the current site.
5. After a change, **Modified · not saved** appears next to the menu: press **Update to current state** to save the new filters, or **Rename** or **Delete view**.

Views are **personal** by default; a **shared** view is visible to every member of the site and is marked with **· Shared**. Only the person who created the view or a site owner can change or delete it: for everyone else a shared view is read-only.

:::caution[If a view does not do what you expect]
- **Name already used:** names must be unique for the same site and the same person; choose another one.
- **Read-only view:** it is shared and you did not create it. To change it, save your own copy with **Save as view**.
- **View no longer available:** it was deleted or you switched site. Reload the page and choose **No view** again.
- **The columns do not change:** sorting and columns are set in **List** mode; the board uses the saved filters but has no columns to choose, and **Light tasks** has fixed columns.
:::

## What is not available yet

- Sharing a view with individual people instead of the whole site.
- Editing a task after creation.
- Acceptance criteria, long descriptions, checklists.
- Deleting a task.
