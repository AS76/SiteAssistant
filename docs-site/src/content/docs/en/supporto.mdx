# Support

For questions, issues or reports, write to **[asassi@asassi.cloud](mailto:asassi@asassi.cloud)**.

To make the request recognisable, put **SiteAssistant** in the subject.

## What to include

- the site or view involved;
- what you were doing and what you expected;
- the error message, if any;
- steps to reproduce the issue where possible.

## What not to send

Do not send passwords, keys, credentials, unnecessary personal data or confidential attachments. In the demo, use synthetic data only. For security or privacy reports, write without attaching sensitive content and say how we can contact you.

## Scope

Support covers the use of SiteAssistant, the documentation and defect reports. It is not legal, tax or security advice and does not authorise changes to data, permissions or environments.

SiteAssistant-related requests may receive an automatic reply. Out-of-scope, sensitive questions or anything requiring a financial, legal or contractual commitment are left to a person. Do not send unnecessary personal data.
