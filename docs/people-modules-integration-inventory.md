## Common reconciliation contract

1. **Preview.** Read by site and source-module permission. Identify an occurrence by record, field/role and source version or digest. Display original values, nulls, status, evidence and the relevant fact date where available. Candidate resolution is same-site and role-specific. Account FKs, scoped source identities and dated relations are evidence. Label similarity is a suggestion. Report coverage and unresolved counts without exposing restricted rows through registry search or exports. (`docs/people-and-companies.md:20–28`, `docs/people-and-companies.md:59–61`, `docs/people-and-companies.md:76–80`, `server/site-registry.mjs:56–65`, `server/site-registry.mjs:83–125`, `server/hr.mjs:40–73`)
2. **Decision.** The authorized reviewer confirms an existing record, approves creation of an explicitly typed record, or records an unresolved reason. Retain source, revision, reviewer, rule, interval, original label and resulting reference. Require separate owner confirmation for account links. Historical attribution requires evidence valid on the fact date, including when an account, affiliation or lead later changes.
